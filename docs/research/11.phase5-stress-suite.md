## Flash-shock injection<a name="flash-shock-injection"></a>

Daily-bar scope, stated up front: an intraday wick that recovers by close is invisible at daily bars, and with zero margin (max gross exposure 0.68×) liquidation is impossible — the binding stress is a **daily-close shock**. We inject a **−30 % book-level day** (harsher than the historical analog: a 2020-03-12-scale −30 % *basket* day at the book's max exposure 0.68× would be −20.4 % book-level) into the capped net-of-cost series and rebuild the governed path **through** the shock (feedback honored: the governor sees the shock in its own equity).

| Injection | Worst governed day | Governed maxDD | Ungoverned maxDD (same shock) | Breaches |
| -- | -- | -- | -- | -- |
| (a) at max exposure (k = 952, mult 1.0) | −27.91 % | 27.92 % | 28.37 % | 1 |
| (b) one bar before the 2023 trough (mid-DD) | **−7.73 %** | **21.21 %** | 48.08 % | 1 |
| (c) inside a 0.25×-rung stretch | **−7.54 %** | 26.36 % | 47.90 % | 1 |
| (d) two consecutive −30 % at max exposure | −27.91 % | **27.92 %** | 49.78 % | 1 |

Readings (all consistent with the governor's spec semantics — no anomaly):

1. **A first shock at full multiplier passes through** ((a): −27.91 % ≈ the injected −30 % less that day's base return). A trailing governor reacts *after* losses; it cannot defend against a single out-of-nowhere day taken at full risk. This instantly exceeds the 15 % budget — the budget governs *paths*, not single-day gaps. For live ops this is the §10 *operational* layer's job (per-short hard stops, watchdog, margin floor), not the drawdown ladder's.
2. **The aftermath is where it earns its keep**: in (d), the second consecutive −30 % day does **zero** additional governed damage — the breach after day one flattens the book (30-bar stand-down), so governed maxDD is identical to (a)'s single-shock case, vs 49.78 % ungoverned. Cascades are cut at one day's depth.
3. **In degraded regimes the ladder pre-positions**: (b) and (c) land while the book already runs at 0.25× — the same shock does −7.5…−7.7 % instead of −28 %, and governed maxDD stays 21–26 % vs ≈ 48 % ungoverned.
4. The per-cycle-budget property (iter-058) manifests as documented: post-breach HWM reset means cumulative cross-cycle DD (27.92 % in (a)) exceeds the 15 % terminal rung. Reported, not excused.

## What the suite establishes<a name="what-the-suite-establishes"></a>

The adopted combined system survives every §12 stress rung on its pre-registered reading. Its two structural costs are now precisely characterized and on the record: (i) no protection against a first-day shock taken at full multiplier — single-day gap risk belongs to the §10 operational layer at Phase 6; (ii) windows that begin inside a bear pay the overlay's de-risk cost without its compounding benefit. Neither moves the adopt verdict (record 33 stands); both belong in the Phase-6 runbook's assumptions section.
