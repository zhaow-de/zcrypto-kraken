## Runbook draft<a name="runbook-draft"></a>

*Draft — finalized at Phase 6 build; the human's go/no-go to paper is the §12 decision point after the holdout look.*

**Daily cycle (once per day, after the 00:00 UTC bar closes):**

1. Pull the day's OHLC for the 10 EUR pairs; append to the working dataset; QA (no gaps, no duplicate timestamps, close > 0).
2. Recompute: inverse-vol weights (30d) → basket return → equity index → 200d gate → 30d vol-target scalar → `p_a = w_a · l3` → `apply_position_caps` → governor multiplier from the live book's own equity (daily-loss state + trailing-HWM ladder) → target positions `mult · c_a`.
3. Diff targets vs held positions; a change beyond the rebalance dead-band (Phase-6 calibration; research charged every diff) becomes maker-first orders per §10 operational rules.
4. Record: equity, HWM, drawdown, active rung, cooldown state, per-asset positions, fees paid — the same quantities the backtest tracks, so live and simulated governors are reconcilable bar-for-bar.

**Governor events (live semantics):**

- Daily loss ≤ −3 %: halve targets, flatten new risk, **flag the human** (§10) — the 5-bar research cooldown is the default pending the flag's resolution.
- Ladder rungs at −7.5 %/−11 %: mechanical, no human gate.
- **−15 % breach: flat + human post-mortem before restart.** The research 30-bar re-arm with HWM reset is *not* the live rule; the post-mortem decides restart and budget. Known property (iter-058, manifest in stress at ×2 fees): a mechanical re-arm makes the 15 % budget per-cycle, so cumulative cross-cycle DD can exceed 15 % — the post-mortem must set the new budget explicitly.

**Standing assumptions & known limits (from the validation record):**

1. **First-shock pass-through** (iter-060): a single-day shock at full multiplier passes through (−27.9 % on a −30 % injected day). The trailing ladder governs paths, not gaps; single-day gap risk belongs to the §10 operational layer (watchdog, margin floor — vacuous while spot-only — and, for any future short, hard stops).
2. **Bear-start windows** (iter-060): a window beginning inside a bear pays the overlay's de-risk cost without its compounding benefit (2021/2022 starts: small-positive → small-negative). Expectation management for a live start date inside a drawdown.
3. **Return give-up**: the combined overlay trades ~0.8 %/yr of return for the DD halving (~0.67 pp from the governor, ~0.15 pp from the cap; both-direction SPA n.s.). The system is Sharpe-superior, return-inferior, to its own ungoverned sleeve.
4. **Cost model headroom**: 0.006/side is a maker × 1.5 convention (40 bps fee + 20 bps headroom). T0014 calibrated the real spread from captured L2 on 2026-07-22 — 2–4 bps/side, i.e. the headroom is 5–10× too large; whether the constant over- or under-charges turned on maker vs taker — **ruled 2026-08-03: maker-first with a taker fallback**, with the realized blend measured at rung 1 (T0090).
5. Turnover-linear cost scaling under the governor overlay (logged iter-058); Phase 6 enforces the rules at weight level, removing the approximation.

**Monitoring hooks (Phase-6 build):** daily governed-vs-target reconciliation; rung-transition and daily-loss-trigger alerts; drawdown vs budget; fee-tier drift vs the cost model; dataset-QA failures; the §10 operational watchdog set.

## Holdout-look protocol (pre-registered — the finding first)<a name="holdout-look-protocol-pre-registered-%E2%80%94-the-finding-first"></a>

**The finding (T0017):** the register-default holdout — *"final 12 months at data freeze"* — was **never carved out**. Every backtest from Phase 3 through record 33 ran the full dataset span (through 2026-03-31); the final 12 in-sample months are burned. The only genuinely untouched data is **out-of-time, after 2026-03-31**. **RATIFIED (human, attended session 2026-07-09, decisions log [iter-072]): Option 1 — the out-of-time window 2026-04-01 → a fresh freeze at the look date.** The look's subject is **record 33** (the one-sleeve system): the T0009 review admitted A1-lf weekly v0.12 (trial 34, adopt) and the two-sleeve combination was built and validated as trial 35, which **rejected** per its pre-registered criteria (Sharpe 1.3135 < record 33's 1.3263; not dominated — maxDD 12.47 % < 14.49 % — but the criteria were fixed first). The original options, for the record:

- **Option 1 (recommended):** holdout = 2026-04-01 → the fresh pre-deployment freeze date. Genuinely clean; the cost is power (a ~4–6-month window's CIs are wide — reported as such, per the exit bar's own "with CIs" wording).
- **Option 2:** delay the event until 12 clean out-of-time months exist (~2027-04). Full pre-registered power; defers deployment ~9 months.
- Using the burned in-sample months is **not** an option.

**The mechanical procedure (fixed now, before any number exists; no parameter may change after seeing any figure):**

1. Human ratifies the window (above); the look is recorded regardless of outcome.
2. Fresh OHLC pull for the 10 EUR pairs through the freeze date; dataset QA; manifest hashed and recorded.
3. Compute **exactly two systems** on the ratified window only: the combined system (spec above, all parameters frozen as of record 33) and the frozen benchmark (§9). Net-of-cost both, same conventions as research.
4. Report: Sharpe both systems; the Sharpe difference with **paired-index stationary-bootstrap CIs** (one index resample applied to *both* series per draw — the pairing convention pinned by the iter-064 dry-run; mean_block 17, n_resamples 2000, seeds 42/7/1234); maxDD both; the governor's engagement evidence on the window. Both series come from one `cli.portfolio.build_combined_system` call (the result carries the system *and* the frozen benchmark).
5. Read the exit bar verbatim: *combined beats or equals the frozen bar, with CIs reported.* "Equals" = the CI on the difference contains 0 with the point estimate ≥ 0 is a pass; a CI entirely below 0 is a fail — recorded either way.
6. Create `docs/research/13.phase5-holdout-ledger.md` (the §9 budgeted-holdout ledger: date, window, dataset hash, present, figures, verdict) and record the look. Budget after this event: **0**.
7. The human's go/no-go-to-paper call (§12 decision point) follows, in their presence, on the recorded numbers.

**Until the event: no performance computation touches post-2026-03-31 data.** Pulling data is not looking; computing on it is.

**Procedure dry-run (iter-064, in-sample — zero holdout risk):** the full CI machinery was exercised end-to-end on the *research* window through the committed builder (QA: frozen figures reproduced first). Annualized Sharpe-difference point +0.0809 full / +0.0830 k≥230; 95 % CIs ≈ [−0.12, +0.29] full and [−0.14, +0.30] k≥230, seed-stable across 42/7/1234, 2000/2000 valid resamples — an in-sample demonstration of the exit bar's *equals* branch (CI contains 0, point ≥ 0), consistent with the both-direction SPA n.s. from record 33. At the event, only the window changes.
