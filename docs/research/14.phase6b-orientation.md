## 1. What §12 SAID 6b was, and why it was not executable as written

*(Superseded 2026-08-02: §12 now specifies the three-rung ladder below. This section records the original and why it fell, because a reader needs to know what changed.)*

§12: *"Stage 6b — Paper-with-teeth / tiny live (2–4 weeks): €500–1,000 live sleeve (real fills, real rollover ledger entries) at full logic, quarter size; weekly tracking-error report vs simulation (…); cost-model recalibration from real fills; **Blockpit T2 sync & classification inspection (§11)** after the first two weeks of sleeve activity, with the T3 fallback built autonomously if it fails."*

**These figures are inherited, not from today's run** — measured **2026-07-30** over the then-current window of **801 intended orders / 120 cycles / 20 days**, recorded in \[[T0116]\]. §4's figures come from the larger 136-cycle journal and are re-measured; these are not, and the two windows are not interchangeable.

- At €500 quarter-size, **0 of 801** intended orders clear Kraken's minimums. At €1,000 full weights, **4.0 %** do. The median intended order is **€0.0116** against per-pair `ordermin` floors of €3–25.
- The blocker is `ordermin` (a **quantity** floor — 20 ADA, 50 DOGE, 0.00005 BTC), not `costmin` (€0.45). Median miss is ~3 orders of magnitude.
- Scaling to mostly-placeable needs ~€228k (50 %), ~€1.36 M (80 %). **The design never produces mostly-placeable orders at any sanctioned size.**

The pre-registered go/no-go band is unmeetable for the same reason — see §4.

## 4. The four measured facts that shape every 6b decision

**(a) The book runs at ~5 % gross, and the chain is now decomposed** (spec `00081`, medians over 136 cycles):

```
A2 sleeve  32.224 %  →  ÷3 (fixed ⅓ weights)  →  10.741 %  →  caps bind 0/136  →  ×0.5 governor  →  5.371 %
```

**(b) Two of the three sleeves hold zero in every cycle.** B and A1 are float-exactly `0.0` in all 136. Not a defect — B was live on 51.4 % of the backtest, A1 on 57.0 %; both went flat ~2025-10-30 / ~2025-11-09, and B sits behind a 200-day gate the holdout ledger already records as off. But the consequence is that **the ⅓ divisor is dividing a one-sleeve book**, so what rung 3 would take live is structurally one sleeve at ~⅙ of A2's gross — not the diversified three-sleeve system whose Sharpe, drawdown and stress figures record 44 registered. This is \[[T0124]\], an owner go/no-go input rather than a bug to fix.

**(c) The ORIGINAL band was unmeetable at rung-3 size, with numbers — and has been replaced.** The old band was `weekly |live − sim| sleeve return ≤ 10 bps of sleeve NAV` (pre-registered in the iter-079 go-live criteria). §12 now reads **the p95 of the measured per-cycle drift floor at the funded NAV, against that week's mean drift, over ≥3 complete ISO weeks** — the numbers below are what that floor measures. Measured drift floor imposed by the venue minimums alone (bps of NAV, per cycle):

| NAV | orders placed | median | p95 |
| -- | -- | -- | -- |
| €500 | 14/136 | 147.0 | 209.2 |
| **€1,000** | 29/136 | **51.2** | **115.7** |
| €2,500 | 40/136 | 11.6 | 30.9 |
| €5,000 | 50/136 | 6.2 | 14.2 |

At rung 3's €1,000 the floor is **5.1× the band at the median, 11.6× at p95**. It only becomes meetable around €5,000 (median) / €10,000 (p95) — the *full* ramp, not the tiny-live size §12 specifies. Minimums stamped `2026-07-07`; \[[T0113]\]'s monthly sweep moves them, so re-read before quoting any of this at a gate.

**(d) (b) IS observed now — this was the gap, and it is closed (2026-08-03).** `zcrypto_engine_active_sleeves` and `zcrypto_engine_sleeve_gross{sleeve}` ship from the engine every cycle, admitted through the capture keep-list and alerting on a count change. First sample read **by value**: `active_sleeves` = **1**, with B and A1 at zero and A2 carrying the book — the one-sleeve state confirmed live rather than inferred. A re-arming would now be announced, not discovered. \[[T0124]\] is resolved on that basis.

## 5. What exists, and what must be built

**Already live and uncommanded:** Nautilus runs a real `TradingNode` — `LiveRiskEngine`, `LiveExecutionEngine`, `Portfolio`, `Cache`, `OrderEmulator` all instantiated; startup reconciliation **fail-closes** the node; authenticated account state streams over an executions WS the engine already holds; the Cache carries live instrument definitions. §12 requires no simulator: *"Kraken has no spot testnet — shadow mode plus tiny-live replaces it."*

**Ours to build (\[[T0018]\]'s executor iteration):** order submission + the order state machine; the reconciliation loop; the kill-switch; order/position/PnL metrics as first-class families (admitted through the Alloy keep-lists *in the same change*); the weekly tracking-error report in full; cost recalibration from real fills; and the four pieces the placeability audit added — the asset→`InstrumentId` map (none exists), venue-constraint sizing, restart→reduce-only **as code**, and fill/fee/rollover ingestion.

**Config gaps that only bite when orders flow (\[[T0120]\]):** `default_leverage` unset, `spot_positions_quote_currency` defaulting to `"USDT"` on a EUR book, and venue open-order/position discrepancy polling **off** — the go/no-go's "zero unreconciled states" needs that polling on to mean anything.

**Risk layer (\[[T0121]\]):** three of §10's four whole-book limits — `apply_gross_leverage_cap`, `apply_net_exposure_band`, `apply_margin_floor` — are implemented, tested, and called by **nothing**. Only `apply_position_caps` is wired. Every design document reads as though the risk layer is operative.

## 7. What to distrust, and re-measure before trusting

- **The band, if anything changes the target series.** It is derived from *shadow* targets. Rung 1/2 execution changing them is the obvious trigger; the sharper ones are a **governor lift off ×0.5** or **either dormant sleeve re-arming**, which move targets with no execution change at all — and fail in a direction that was ASSUMED and has since been measured: the reasoning was that a bigger book shrinks the relative floor, so a stale band would be too *wide* and would pass execution it should fail. Measured at the 2026-08-22 sleeve reversal, the p95 floor moved the other way and the stale band is too *narrow* there — it fails safe. Re-derive rather than reason about the direction. **The 2026-08-03 §12 amendment adds one more trigger — whenever the realized maker/taker blend moves — and the band's two uncertainties (statistical CI, cost basis) are now stated as separate terms, never merged into one interval.**
- **The minimums stamp.** `2026-07-07`. Kraken moves these without notice.
- **`14.phase6b-session-brief.md`'s decision queue.** Items 1–6 describe the un-sub-phased 6b and predate the ladder; items 7–10 and its "already ruled" section are still live. Its §1 gate figures are from streak 12/14 — stale by construction, and it says so.
- **Any Sharpe quoted for the deployable.** Record 44's headline 1.5609 is a ×1.0-cost figure. Measured across the execution range 2026-08-03: headline **1.6304** maker → 1.5609 registered → **1.3641** taker; conditional live-state **0.9072 → 0.7509 → 0.5066**. Execution is **ruled maker-first with a taker fallback**, so the basis starts near the maker end and the realized blend is measured at rung 1 — until then the quoted baseline is a **range, not a point**. The **43-vs-44 ordering is not merely unread but UNREADABLE** — trial 43's construction was never committed and its scratchpad runner is gone, so the criterion that selected the deployable can never be re-examined at a different cost basis (\[[T0125]\]). ## 8. Method note

This document was written after spec `00081`'s measurements landed, so §4 quotes them at full precision from the runs rather than from the topics that requested them. Two of its statements correct earlier drafts of my own: the ~5 % gross was previously attributed to sleeve *cancellation* (spec `00081` D3 called that "the likeliest answer"), which the measurement refuted — the sleeve→combined ratio is 1.000 because two sleeves are zero, not because they disagree; and an earlier write-up called that ratio "exactly 1.000", which is false at float precision. The exactness belongs to the zero, not the ratio. Both corrections are recorded where they occurred rather than quietly applied.
