1. **The A-family arc (iters 040–053).** Leak-tested feature substrate (`cli/features/`) → the A1 book + kill-bar harness (`cli/alpha/`) → A1's literal-bar survivor (family SPA p = 0.011) → the **net-of-cost reality** (gated-B1 1.047 beat every survivor; the short's ~5 %/yr borrow carry identified as the structural killer over three independent confirmations, A2's short arms the third — B4 later added a fourth, iter-055) → cost-optimization ruled out (cadence can't touch a holding cost) → the long/flat capstone (net-of-cost superior, 2014-fragile) → A2's Donchian test (premise real — ~1.6–2.5× cheaper turnover — but no significant beat on the corrected window). Registry: 32 trials, every one with hypothesis/hashes/metrics/verdict.
2. **The benchmark family raised its own bar (iters 044, 055).** The full-history dynamic basket (T0007) answered finding-1 (basket ≈ BTC); the recovered T0010 deferral then produced **B3+vt-dynamic**, point-superior to gated-B1 net-of-cost — adopted by the human as the frozen benchmark. No alpha family was needed to move the bar; §9's "best of {benchmarks ∪ survivors}" worked as designed.
3. **Four instrument findings, all now codified** (`.claude/skills/research-loop/references/case-log.md`): the inert DSR gate (fixed pre-verdict), the exposure-blind worst-slice leg (fails the benchmark itself — T0009), the benchmark warm-up window asymmetry (T0009), and the zero-fee SPA cost-asymmetry gap (tool shipped: `net_of_cost_verdict`; protocol fold-in — T0009). Plus the process finding that produced the deferral-registration discipline (the lost iter-045 worst-slice flag; `ripe_when:` triggers now mandatory).
4. **Harness deliverables beyond the families**: `net_of_cost_verdict`, `benchmark_relative_worst_slice`, registry schema v3 (first-class `variant`, T0013), the per-asset turnover + margin-carry cost model, and the offset-averaged cadence method.
