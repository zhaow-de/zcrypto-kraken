1. **The §10 risk layer as tested code** (`cli/risk/`): the drawdown governor (improves Sharpe while cutting maxDD 43 % on the candidate; no knife-edge in ±25 % sensitivity) and the per-asset cap (binds ~2–3 % of bars). Two structural properties documented and stress-verified: first-shock pass-through (single-day gap risk belongs to the operational layer) and the per-cycle budget under mechanical re-arm.
2. **The combination trials**: the one-sleeve P1 (record 33, **adopt**) and — after the attended review admitted A1-lf weekly v0.12 (trial 34, **adopt** under the ratified bar) — the two-sleeve P1 (trial 35, **reject** per pre-registered criteria: Sharpe 1.3135 < 1.3263; *not dominated* — maxDD 12.47 % — with a DD-aware adopt criterion now pre-registered for trial 36+).
3. **The ratified validation protocol** (T0009, attended 2026-07-09, all six legs) — in code (`a1_kill_bar`): net-of-cost SPA on the k≥230 decisive window, benchmark-relative + stub-excluded worst-slice, DSR 0.95; master-plan §12 rewritten, decision-dated.
4. **The holdout discipline repaired and exercised**: the never-carved-out register default surfaced (T0017), an honest out-of-time window ratified, the look executed once in the human's presence, ledgered, budget spent.
5. **Reproducibility as standing tests**: the committed builder (`cli/portfolio/`) reproduces record 33's figures and trial 34's verdict as skipif-data regression tests (931 total).
6. **Instrument catches, all pre-verdict**: the A2 double-division (~1/19-scale books in two probe drivers), the sleeve-weight degenerate-window under-specification (driver/diagnostic bisection), the kill-bar resample-count hardcoding, and the spec-hash provenance gap (disclosed) — every one caught by review or QA before a verdict flowed through it.
