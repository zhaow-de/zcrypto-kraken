[iter-030] Phase 3 — B2 inverse-vol basket: composition + calendar. (Decision: 2)
The 10 EUR majors share an end (2026-03-31) but very different starts (BTC/LTC 2013 → SOL 2021-06 → AVAX
2021-12). Two ways to build the inverse-vol basket:
  1. **Dynamic-composition basket on BTC's full calendar** — align all assets to the 2013→2026 span, mark
     pre-listing periods absent, and each day inverse-vol-weight over only the currently-listed-and-warmed-up
     assets (basket grows 2→10 assets over time). Most realistic (uses all history, mirrors how you'd actually
     hold a live basket) and preserves the 12-yr horizon — but variable-width with presence masks and per-day
     renormalization over a changing set: the highest look-ahead / edge-case surface, riskiest to get right at
     deep context.
  2. **Fixed 10-asset basket on the common window (intersection, 2021-12-21 → 2026-03-31, ~1560 days)** —
     all 10 present every day, inverse-vol weighted, look-ahead-free; no masks. Simplest defensible + most

     SAME window, and the window still spans a full bull→bear→recovery cycle (2021 peak, 2022 LUNA/FTX crash,
     2023-25 recovery). Cost: only ~4.3 yr, drops BTC's 2013-2020 history for the basket. Recommended at deep
     context + the ~2h gate — the correctness-critical piece (look-ahead-free inverse-vol weighting) is
     isolated from the composition-churn complexity.
  3. Small fixed universe over a long window (e.g. BTC+ETH+LTC+XRP from 2017) — compromise, but an arbitrary
     universe cut and still not the full 10; neither maximally-realistic nor maximally-simple.
  (Decision: 2) — fixed 10-asset intersection basket: isolate the look-ahead-critical inverse-vol weighting

[iter-033] Phase 3 — quantify benchmark-family uncertainty (bootstrap CIs) + FREEZE the deployment bar.
The §12 Phase-3 exit bar requires "B3/B4 numbers with bootstrap CIs" + "deployment bar = best benchmark,
frozen in the decision log." Applied `cli.validation.bootstrap_ci` (stationary block bootstrap, Politis-Romano)
to each strategy's net-return series — statistic = annualized Sharpe, n_resamples=2000, alpha=0.05, seed=42,
mean block ℓ≈n^(1/3) (16 for the 4580-day BTC history, 12 for the 1561-day common window). Findings (robust to
ℓ∈{8,16,24} and seed∈{1,42,99}):
  - **Full-history BTC family:** all four Sharpes are significantly positive (95% lower bounds ~0.43–0.61) but
    their CIs OVERLAP heavily — the differences are NOT statistically distinguishable from a single ~12-yr path.
    gated-B1 has both the highest point (1.247) AND the highest lower bound (~0.60, robustly >0).
  - **Common-window family (4.3 yr):** EVERY CI straddles zero — not one strategy has a significantly-positive
    Sharpe over this short window (B0 [-0.55,1.38], B2 [-0.74,1.18], B4 [-1.05,0.80]); the basket
    "underperformance" and B4's "disaster" are directional point estimates, not significant. Strengthens the case
    for T0007's full-history basket (this window lacks the power to conclude anything).
  (Decision: FREEZE the deployment bar = **gated-B1** — vol-targeted BTC (10%/yr, 30d) × the 200-day long/flat
  regime gate. Full-history point Sharpe 1.247 (95% CI ~[0.61, 1.88], robustly >0), maxDD 12.3%, ~1.12 after
  Tier-1 fees (survives §9.6 to 2× costs). Chosen on its highest point estimate + highest/robustly-positive
  lower-bound Sharpe + by far the best drawdown control (12% vs B0's 82%) — NOT on a statistically-significant
  Sharpe edge over B0/B1, which the CIs show does not exist. Any Phase-4 alpha must beat this bar net of stressed
  costs to justify added complexity. B4 (short) is characterized as the fallback and is NOT acceptable on its
  own — basket B4 Sharpe -0.136 (whipsawed); the human decision point (§12: "confirm B4's risk behavior") stays
  open for phase close-out.)
