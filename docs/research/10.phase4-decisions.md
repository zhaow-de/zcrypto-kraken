[iter-045] Kill-bar DSR leg — how to operationalize the master-plan's pre-registered "DSR > 0" against this codebase's probability-valued DSR. (Decision: dsr > 0.5, faithful to "DSR > 0"; + units fix)
  The final whole-branch review caught a CRITICAL instrument bug (exactly the "distrust the instrument" failure mode): the kill-bar's DSR leg was coded `dsr > 0`, but `cli/validation/deflated_sharpe_ratio` returns a PROBABILITY in [0,1] (PSR with the deflated benchmark = expected_max_sharpe(n_trials, var_trials)), so `dsr > 0` is trivially true — 20/20 null (beta=0) books passed the DSR leg; only SPA was discriminating. Compounded by a units bug: a1_kill_bar computed a PER-PERIOD sharpe but the tests passed an annualized-scale var_trials=1.0, making the deflation benchmark SR*≈1.80 per-period (≈34 annualized) — unbeatable — so at real iter-046 scale (n_obs≈2500) DSR underflowed to exactly 0.0 and the leg would REJECT EVERY variant unconditionally (a silent false-reject of honest edges). Caught in iter-045 (harness build) before any verdict was read.
  Threshold question: the master plan §12 kill bar says "DSR > 0". That phrasing is the Z-score/deflated-SR convention (deflated Sharpe point estimate > 0). Against this codebase's probability-valued DSR = P(true SR > SR*_deflated), "deflated point estimate > 0" ⟺ P > 0.5. Options: (1) **dsr > 0.5 (chosen)** — the FAITHFUL operationalization of the pre-registered "DSR > 0" (the deflated Sharpe point estimate is positive after 16-trial multiple-testing deflation); SPA-beats-gated-B1 carries the significance-vs-benchmark burden as its own separate kill-bar condition, so DSR need not double it. (2) dsr > 0.95 — the conventional López-de-Prado DSR significance bar; stricter than the pre-registered "DSR > 0" (would add a 95%-confidence requirement the master plan did not specify) — noted as the alternative the human can ratify at the Phase-5 combination review if a stricter multiple-testing bar is wanted. Chose the faithful (1): run the pre-registered bar on its stated meaning, don't invent extra stringency autonomously. Reversible — only affects iter-046's (re-runnable) verdict.
  Units fix (chosen alongside): keep sr = sharpe(book_net_returns) PER-PERIOD (PSR's formula is per-observation with n_obs = len(returns)); require var_trials in per-period Sharpe² units (at iter-046 = variance of the 16 variants' per-period Sharpes); document the contract in a1_kill_bar's docstring + guard an implausible per-period var_trials. [iter-046] Worst-slice kill-bar leg — how to handle a zero-variance (all-flat) regime year-slice. (Decision: skip zero-variance slices, like length<2)
