# Phase 4 Orientation — Alpha Sprints, Informed by the Phase-3 Benchmark Findings

**Phase:** 4 (Alpha Research Sprints) · **Status:** orientation memo (interim; not a phase close-out — no decision-log drain) · **Written:** 2026-07-08

This memo orients the Phase-4 kickoff. It condenses the §5 ranked alpha queue and — its real purpose — folds the **hard empirical findings from the just-closed Phase 3** into the design of the first family (A1), so the alpha sprint starts from what the benchmarks actually showed rather than from the plan's priors alone (§5: EV is "assessed against the prior phases' negative results"). It is a fresh-context handoff: a later session can pick up A1's spec directly from the "A1 kickoff" section below.

## The bar every Phase-4 family must beat

Frozen in Phase 3 (`docs/research/04.phase3-benchmarks-closeout.md`): **gated-B1** — vol-targeted BTC (10%/yr, 30-day, ≤1.0×) × the 200-day long/flat regime gate. Full-history Sharpe **1.247** (95% CI ~[0.61, 1.88]), maxDD **12.3%**, ~1.12 after Tier-1 fees, worst calendar year **−5.5%**. The §9 deployment rule: the deployed system is the best of {benchmarks ∪ validated survivors}, so an alpha earns its place only by beating this bar **net of stressed costs and at its trial count** (DSR/SPA), not on a point estimate.

## The §5 ranked queue (condensed)

- **Bucket A (build first).** **A1** — long/flat/short regime overlay on a vol-targeted majors basket (extends the survivor: add a short state, an ensemble regime signal, book-level vol-targeting). **A2** — per-asset symmetric TSMOM ensemble (Donchian/multi-lookback, inverse-vol aggregated). A1 vs A2 is an **A/B inside one family**, not two families.
- **Bucket B (new ground, strict rigor).** B1 intraday trend + seasonality (1h/4h); B2 intraday derivatives-positioning features (funding/OI/liquidations, free data); B3 simple cross-sectional long-only tilt + BTC-short overlay (low prior — priors refuted ML/quantile L·S); B4 relative-value trend on crypto/crypto ratio legs (ETH/BTC, SOL/BTC).
- **Bucket C (long shots, fast kills).** C1 short-term reversal (needs captured L2 → deferred); C2 BTC→alt intraday lead-lag; C3 slow on-chain regime features.

## What Phase 3 changed about the A1 design (the value of this memo)

Four Phase-3 results bear directly on A1 and should reshape it before a single feature is built:

1. **The majors basket *underperformed* single-asset BTC — so A1's "vol-targeted majors basket" base is not a free improvement.** Phase-3 B2 (naive inverse-vol basket of the 10 majors) *lost* money over its common window (0.68×, Sharpe 0.194, maxDD 69%), worse than BTC alone, because 8 of 10 majors ended 47–95% down in the 2022 alt-crash and inverse-vol weighting still holds the losers. **Implication:** A1 must not assume the basket beats BTC. Test the basket base as an explicit A/B against a BTC-anchored book; if the basket cannot clear BTC-only in-sample, the "majors basket" premise is refuted and A1 collapses toward the single-asset survivor. The basket's diversification value must be *re-earned*, not assumed. (B2's underperformance was over a single BTC-unfavorable window, so it is not yet established whether the basket's weakness is *structural* — alt-vs-BTC beta — or a *window artifact*; that plus the power problem in finding 4 means this A/B needs the full-history universe, cf. open topic **T0007**.)

2. **Naive lagging-gate *shorting* was disastrous — A1's short state must be built to the §5 spec, not as a gate flip.** Phase-3 B4 (basket long/short via the 200-day gate flipping to short below the SMA) had **negative** Sharpe (−0.136), maxDD 83% — the lagging gate shorts *after* the crash and is whipsawed by the violent 2022–23 bear rallies. §5 already warns "the short extension must not soften [the gate] into a whipsaw machine." **Implication:** A1's short state must use the §5 design — enter short only on *confirmed* bear (price < slow SMA − band **and** negative multi-horizon momentum), at ≤0.5× the long exposure, with the carry-hurdle test applied at entry — and the kill bar must explicitly test whipsaw (turnover, hit-rate, and drawdown of the short leg in isolation). A short state that merely mirrors the long gate is pre-refuted by B4.

3. **On the basket, the regime gate was *redundant* with vol-targeting — A1's ensemble must beat the single gate to justify itself.** Phase-3 found gated-B2 + vol-target (Sharpe 0.270) was *worse* than plain B2 + vol-target (0.453): once vol-targeting controlled the risk, the gate only subtracted participation — the opposite of BTC, where gate × vol-target (gated-B1) was the family's best. **Implication:** A1's "small ensemble (BTC regime + per-asset trend agreement)" is not automatically additive. A/B it against (a) the single BTC gate and (b) vol-targeting alone; keep it only if the ensemble's regime signal beats both. Complexity that doesn't beat the single gate is YAGNI.

4. **Estimation uncertainty is large — beating the bar's *point* estimate is not enough.** Phase-3 bootstrap CIs showed the full-history BTC benchmark Sharpes are mutually indistinguishable (CIs overlap), and the 4.3-year common-window family is *entirely* within noise (every CI straddles zero). **Implication:** A1's edge over gated-B1 must survive the kill bar's DSR (> 0 at the trial count) *and* SPA (beats the frozen benchmark accounting for multiple looks) *and* 1.5× cost stress *and* the worst walk-forward regime slice — a point-estimate uplift over a single path is exactly what the CIs warn is noise. Register every A1/A2 variant as a trial (the registry's first real records) so the trial count is honest.

## A1 kickoff (the first Phase-4 iteration to spec)

**Hypothesis:** a vol-targeted, long/flat/short trend/regime book over the majors — extending the frozen survivor with a disciplined short state and an ensemble regime signal — beats gated-B1 net of 1.5× stressed costs, at its registered trial count.

**Leak-free feature construction (all strictly causal, per §6's rules):** multi-horizon momentum/returns (4h→90d), breakout/channel distance, per-asset trend agreement, realized-vol state (for the book-level vol target), BTC-regime state, drawdown state. Reuse the Phase-2/3 primitives (`returns_from_prices`, `vol_target`, `sma_gate`, `run_backtest`) and the look-ahead conventions already reviewed there.

**Structure to build + A/B:** (i) base = BTC-anchored vs equal-risk majors basket (finding 1); (ii) regime = single BTC gate vs ensemble (finding 3); (iii) short state = off vs §5-confirmed-bear at ≤0.5× (finding 2); (iv) book vol-targeted to 10–12%. Each toggle is a registered trial.

**Verdict protocol (§12 Phase-4 kill bar):** CPCV + walk-forward net of stressed costs; a variant is archived unless DSR > 0 at its trial count **and** SPA beats gated-B1 **and** it survives 1.5× cost stress **and** the worst walk-forward regime slice is not disqualifying. Escalate to the human only for: new data spend, abandoning Bucket A early, budget expansion, or any holdout temptation.
