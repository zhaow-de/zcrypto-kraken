## Carried forward — exit-bar remainder (parked)

- **T0003 — D2 forward-capture pipeline (VPS daemon → workstation sync → NAS).** The hard exit-bar gate; human-gated on VPS provisioning, then autonomous. Its ≥7-day clean-run is a wall-clock requirement — the sooner it starts, the sooner the Phase-2 cost model's captured-spread calibration (itself needing ≥2 weeks of spreads) has data.
- **T0004 — full tick history + tick-derived bar reconciliation.** Blocked on tick-data acquisition (not on the NAS; prefer a manual per-pair tick-CSV download mirroring the OHLCVT path, else a bounded resumable REST Trades backfill). Feeds Phase-4 microstructure features + a true (tick-weighted) vwap; deferred to when that substrate is load-bearing.
- **T0005 — Blockpit T1 tax check + memo.** Human-gated *in practice* (the plan labels T1 autonomous, but the Blockpit depot authorize step needs an interactive login this agent can't perform); feeds the after-tax view, not the research harness.
- **Finer cadences (1m/5m/15m).** A deliberate iter-008 non-goal; the 1-minute source is on the NAS, so these are autonomously buildable when needed. Primary relevance is Phase-6 execution (15m scheduler) and Phase-4 microstructure — deferred to a future phase, not a current-phase miss.

## Why Phase 2 proceeds now

Phase 2 (Validation Harness & Cost Model First) is, by the master plan's design, *"proven on synthetic data before any strategy exists"* — its real dependencies are the **trial registry** (Phase 0, `cli/registry/`) and the **canonical 1h/4h/1d datasets** (Phase 1 core, delivered above), both met. The carried-forward items feed **live/execution/microstructure** needs (Phases 4/6) and the **captured-spread** term of the cost model — and that term is itself wall-clock-gated on T0003 (≥2 weeks of captured spreads), so it must fold in later regardless. The cost model's *structure* (fee ladder incl. the July-9 AoP schedule from T0000, margin open + 4-hourly rollover accrual, borrow switch) is buildable and unit-testable on synthetic/assumed spreads now, with captured-spread calibration deferred to when T0003 has run. The master plan itself frames VPS provisioning as a parallel human side-action (*"everything after credentials exist is autonomous"*), so the capture/infra track is designed to run **alongside** research, not serially block it.

**For the human (surfaced, not gating):** provisioning the Linode VPS (T0003) and authorizing the Blockpit depot (T0005) are the two account actions that unblock the carried-forward exit-bar items; T0004's cleanest path is a manual tick-CSV download to the NAS. None block Phase 2. *Process note (for future phases):* VPS provisioning is a Phase-1 **kickoff** issue whose 7-day capture run is wall-clock-gated — ideally it is requested at phase start, not surfaced at close-out as here; future phases should raise such account actions up front.
