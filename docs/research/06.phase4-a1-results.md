## Summary<a name="summary"></a>

> **Correction (iter-047, see `07.phase4-a1-cost-reality.md`):** the "A1 beats gated-B1" result below holds **only zero-fee and per the literal pre-registered bar**. Net of realistic cost — even spot-turnover-only, and more so with short-borrow carry — gated-B1 (net-of-cost Sharpe 1.047) **decisively beats** every A1 survivor (0.40–0.55), because the A1 book's daily 10-asset rebalancing is far more expensive than gated-B1's simple BTC gate. The kill bar's SPA-on-zero-fee + cost-survive-in-isolation legs have a **cost-asymmetry gap** that over-credited A1. **A1 does not raise the deployable bar; gated-B1 remains the deployable system.** *(Update iter-055: 'gated-B1 remains the bar' is itself superseded as settled fact — B3+vt-dynamic point-beats it net-of-cost, n.s.; the bar choice was T0009's first item — decided 2026-07-09: B3+vt-dynamic adopted (master-plan §9). See `07` and `docs/research/04.phase3-benchmark-b2-dynamic-report.md` §B3/B4.)* The `adopt` records below stand as an accurate audit of the *literal-bar* outcome; the *deployment* reading is corrected in `07`.

A1 — the first Bucket-A alpha family — is a **vol-targeted per-asset long/flat/short trend book** over the 10 EUR majors, evaluated over the full 2013→2026 history against the frozen benchmark **gated-B1** (vol-targeted BTC × 200-day regime gate; full-history Sharpe 1.247). Sixteen toggle combinations (base × regime × short × vol-target) were each run through the pre-registered Phase-4 kill bar — **DSR > 0 (deflated for 16 trials) AND SPA beats gated-B1 AND survives 1.5× cost stress AND worst walk-forward regime slice not disqualifying** — and each recorded as a trial in the registry (`docs/reference/trial-registry.jsonl`, A1's 16 are the registry's first real records).

**Verdict: A1 has a validated survivor.** Two variants — **`equal_risk_basket / ensemble / confirmed_bear`** at vol-target 10 % and 12 % — clear all four kill-bar legs, and the multiplicity-corrected **family SPA — White's Reality Check (`reality_check_pvalue`, the conservative sibling of Hansen's SPA) over all 16 variants vs gated-B1 — gives p = 0.011** (robust across bootstrap seeds 0.009–0.0125 and block lengths 0.0095–0.011): the best-of-16 significantly beats gated-B1 **after** correcting for the 16-variant search — not a multiple-testing artifact. All four Phase-3 findings are validated in the process.

This is a **research finding** (the pre-registered kill bar is run autonomously), **not a deployment decision**. Whether to deploy, and the Phase-5 combination of survivors (which consumes the single holdout look), remain the human's calls; the holdout is untouched.

## The benchmark and the construction<a name="the-benchmark-and-the-construction"></a>

- **Frozen benchmark — gated-B1**: `position = sma_gate(BTC, 200) · vol_target(BTC_returns, 10 %/yr, 30d)`, applied to BTC returns, zero-fee. Reproduced here at **Sharpe 1.244** over the union calendar (vs the known 1.247; the 0.003 gap is BTC's single forward-filled 2018-01-12 gap — immaterial). This QA confirms the benchmark is built right before any A1 comparison.
- **A1 book** (`cli/alpha/a1.py`, iter-045): per asset `i`, a direction `d_i[k] ∈ {+1 long, 0 flat, −0.5 short}` from the BTC 200-day market gate (long), the asset's own `trend_agreement` (ensemble confirmation), and a confirmed-bear short (`BTC < SMA·(1−band) AND trend_agreement_i < 0`); aggregated by inverse-vol weights on the union calendar; then vol-targeted through the backtester. Look-ahead-free (leak-tested at the book level).
- **Toggles** (2×2×2×2 = 16 trials, within the A = 40 budget): `base ∈ {btc_only, equal_risk_basket}`, `regime ∈ {single_gate, ensemble}`, `short ∈ {off, confirmed_bear}`, `vol_target ∈ {10 %, 12 %}`. `equal_risk_basket` consumes the full-history dynamic basket validated in iter-044 (finding-1: the basket is co-equal with BTC, so it is carried, not dropped).
- **Cost stress**: the honest **per-asset net-position turnover** each period × **1.5× Tier-1 maker fee (0.60 %)** — not a book-level under-count. **Regime slices**: calendar years (zero-variance warm-up / fully-gated-out years are skipped — the book took no risk there). **DSR** `var_trials` = the per-period variance of the 16 books' Sharpes (5.83e-5).

## The four findings, validated<a name="the-four-findings-validated"></a>

- **Finding 1 (basket base).** The `equal_risk_basket` variants dominate: the top-6 by out-performance are all basket. iter-044 established the basket is co-equal with BTC over the full history; here, *combined with the ensemble regime and the confirmed-bear short*, the basket base is what clears the bar. `btc_only` never passes.
- **Finding 2 (confirmed-bear short — not a gate flip).** The short **rescues the worst regime**: for `equal_risk_basket/ensemble`, 2014's Sharpe flips from **−0.092** (short off — a disqualifying year) to **+0.019** (confirmed_bear). This is exactly finding-2's premise: a *properly-confirmed* short (BTC below its SMA-band **and** the asset's own trend agreeing) adds drawdown-robustness, unlike the naked lagging-gate flip that made benchmark B4 disastrous (Sharpe −0.136). Note the higher-raw-Sharpe `off` variants (1.54–1.57) **fail** the kill bar precisely on their negative-2014 worst slice — the short trades ~0.2 Sharpe of headline return for the regime-robustness that clears the bar.
- **Finding 3 (ensemble beats the single gate).** The `ensemble` variants (BTC gate **and** per-asset trend agreement) out-Sharpe and out-return their `single_gate` siblings across the board — the ensemble justifies itself.
- **Finding 4 (estimation uncertainty / full bar + multiplicity).** No variant is adopted on a point estimate: adoption required the full DSR + SPA + cost + worst-slice bar **and** the survivor survives the multiplicity-corrected family SPA.

## Distrust the instrument (a surprising POSITIVE gets a symmetric bug-hunt)<a name="distrust-the-instrument-a-surprising-positive-gets-a-symmetric-bug-hunt"></a>

A result that beats the frozen bar is scrutinized as hard as a surprising kill. Cleared four ways:

1. **Benchmark built right** — gated-B1 reproduces its known full-history Sharpe (1.244 vs 1.247).
2. **The edge is genuine strategy alpha, not a construction artifact.** A1 vol-targets the *assembled book*, whereas gated-B1 vol-targets raw BTC then gates — a different placement. This placement, if anything, *handicaps* A1: A1's `btc_only/single_gate/off` (Sharpe **1.159**) is *worse* than gated-B1 (1.244). So the winner's out-performance comes from the basket + ensemble + short, not from where vol-targeting is applied.
3. **The decisive p-value is robust** — family SPA p = 0.011 is stable across 5 bootstrap seeds (0.009–0.0125) and 4 block lengths (0.0095–0.011).
4. **DSR passing all 16 is correct, not inert.** The 16 are all genuinely gated-B1-level strategies (per-period Sharpes tightly clustered, `var_trials` 5.83e-5), so the deflation benchmark is low and they all clear it — DSR confirms they are not noise; the binding legs were SPA-vs-benchmark and the worst slice. (The DSR leg itself was fixed in iter-045 from an inert `>0` to a real `>0.5` gate before this run.)

## Honest caveats<a name="honest-caveats"></a>

- **The significant edge is on RETURN, not risk-adjusted headline.** The SPA tests mean out-performance; the winner beats gated-B1 on return significantly. Its **Sharpe edge is modest** — 1.36 vs 1.24, well within the ~1.3-wide bootstrap Sharpe CI (finding-4) — and it takes more risk (maxDD 15.7 % vs 12.3 %). "A1 beats gated-B1" means *significantly higher return at a comparable, marginally-better Sharpe*, not a decisive risk-adjusted win.
- **The passers clear the worst-slice leg thinly.** Their worst risk-taking year (2019) has a per-period Sharpe of only **+0.002** (10 % vol) / **+0.005** (12 % vol) — positive, so non-disqualifying, but barely. A worse draw in any single future regime year could flip the leg; worth weighing at Phase-5.
- **Cost model** = per-asset spot turnover at 1.5× Tier-1 maker. Spot-margin **borrow carry** on the (≤0.5×, active ~33–38 % of periods) short leg is a refinement not yet applied — it would slightly reduce the confirmed_bear variants' edge.
- **Short-band = 0** in this run (the knob exists but defaulted off). A protective band could further improve the short's whipsaw robustness — a cheap follow-up sweep.
- **Zero-fee benchmark comparison** (the SPA compares zero-fee book vs zero-fee gated-B1, consistent with the benchmark family); the cost-stress leg is the separate realistic check.

## Verdict and next steps<a name="verdict-and-next-steps"></a>

- **Recorded**: 16 A1 trials in `docs/reference/trial-registry.jsonl` — **2 `adopt`** (equal_risk_basket/ensemble/confirmed_bear at 10 % and 12 %), **14 `reject`**. A1 is the first Bucket-A family with validated survivors.
- **Human-gated (escalated in the hand-back)**: whether these survivors are "good enough to deploy", and the **Phase-5 combination** of survivors (which consumes the one holdout look). No holdout was touched; nothing is deployed.
- **Autonomous follow-ups**: a short-band sweep and a spot-margin-carry cost refinement on the confirmed_bear variants; **A2** (per-asset TSMOM ensemble) is a separate queued Bucket-A A/B; a full-history dynamic **B3/B4** (gate×basket, basket+short) remains noted from iter-044.
