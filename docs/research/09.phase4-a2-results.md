## Summary<a name="summary"></a>

> **Update (iter-055, see `docs/research/04.phase3-benchmark-b2-dynamic-report.md` §"B3 and B4").** The catch-up run of the long-lost T0010 deferral produced a benchmark that **point-beats gated-B1 net-of-cost**: B3+vt-dynamic (the basket's own 200-day equity gate × vol-target) at **1.245 full / 1.278 post-warm-up vs gated-B1's 1.047 / 1.074** — though the Sharpe edge is **not significant** (p ≈ 0.27) and it carries ~1.8× the drawdown (21.9 % vs 12.3 %). Statements below that "gated-B1 remains the deployable system/bar" are therefore **superseded as settled fact**: the deployable-bar choice (and the kill bar's frozen-benchmark swap, a pre-registered-protocol change) is escalated as the first item of **T0009** **(decided 2026-07-09 on the PR-74 review: B3+vt-dynamic adopted — construction + figures in master-plan §9 and T0009's Done-so-far)**. Notable re-read: **A1-long/flat weekly still significantly beats even the new candidate bar** (offset-mean net-of-cost 1.416 vs 1.278 post-warm-up, p = 0.0070).

A2 — a per-asset **Donchian breakout ensemble** (hold until the opposite channel breaks), inverse-vol aggregated, vol-targeted — was run as 8 registered trials over the full 2013→2026 majors, judged **net-of-cost first** against the frozen benchmark **gated-B1**. Per master-plan §5 A2 is an A/B *inside* the A family (24 of A=40 spent on A1; these 8 take it to 32; 8 held in reserve).

**Verdict: A2 does not beat gated-B1 on the corrected, apples-to-apples test. gated-B1 remains the deployable bar.** But the run produced three results that matter more than the headline:

1. **A2's premise is real: Donchian is genuinely cheaper.** Spot-turnover drag **2.62–2.86 %/yr** (slow lookbacks, long/flat) vs the A1 survivor's **6.47 %/yr** — about **2.5× cheaper**. (That A1 figure is the *with-short* `confirmed_bear` survivor, the actual A1 candidate. Toggle-matched, A1 `short=off` is **4.96 %/yr**, so slow-Donchian is still **~1.6–1.9× cheaper** — the qualitative claim is robust either way.)
2. **The short kills it, again** — a third independent confirmation. `short="on"` carries **6.24–8.50 %/yr** margin drag and its net-of-cost Sharpe collapses to **0.61–0.70**, far below gated-B1's 1.047.
3. **Two instrument findings** (below) — one of which shows the kill bar's worst-slice leg **cannot discriminate a challenger from the benchmark**, and forces a re-scoping of how iter-049's 2014 rejection was expressed.

## Instrument finding 1 — the worst-slice leg disqualifies the benchmark itself<a name="instrument-finding-1-%E2%80%94-the-worst-slice-leg-disqualifies-the-benchmark-itself"></a>

All 8 A2 trials fail the pre-registered kill bar, every one of them on the **worst-slice** leg. That looked like a clean kill. It is not — it is uninformative.

The leg as implemented is **absolute**: *every* non-degenerate calendar-year slice must have Sharpe > 0. Applied to the frozen benchmark:

| gated-B1 calendar year | 2014 | 2018 | 2021 | 2022 | 2025 |
| -- | -: | -: | -: | -: | -: |
| per-period Sharpe | **−0.108** | −0.076 | −0.002 | −0.081 | −0.027 |

**gated-B1 has five negative years and would fail its own worst-slice leg** (worst: 2014, per-period −0.108, annualized −2.07). A leg that disqualifies the bar-setter cannot discriminate a challenger from it. So "0/8 A2 trials fail the kill bar" carries **no information about A2 vs gated-B1**.

Worse, the leg is **exposure-blind**. In 2014 gated-B1's gate held it flat ~87 % of the year (mean exposure **0.034**); it lost only **−5.5 %** with a **6.0 %** drawdown. Its −2.07 Sharpe is an *exposure-normalization artifact* — a near-flat book's tiny choppy returns make a bad ratio out of a negligible loss. A Sharpe-based slice leg therefore punishes prudent non-participation and cannot express the very robustness it appears to test.

There is a compounding asymmetry: the leg **skips zero-variance slices**, so a book that *sits a regime out* is exempt, while one that *trades it* is judged. A1's `short="off"` book was gated flat through 2026 (slice skipped); A2 holds positions through it and books −0.191. The leg silently rewards non-participation.

**The bar was not weakened.** `a1_kill_bar` is untouched and the failures are recorded as they came. Making the leg benchmark-relative (or drawdown-based, or excluding partial-year stubs like the 89-period 2026) is a change to the **pre-registered protocol** — a human Phase-5 call, escalated, not taken here.

## Instrument finding 2 — a benchmark warm-up handicap inflated A2's headline<a name="instrument-finding-2-%E2%80%94-a-benchmark-warm-up-handicap-inflated-a2s-headline"></a>

The first-pass net-of-cost result was a significant win. Symmetric skepticism says a surprising *win* gets the same bug-hunt as a surprising kill — and it did not survive one.

gated-B1's 200-day `sma_gate` is **structurally flat for its first 199 periods**, including all of 2013. A2 has no such gate: its 20-period Donchian trades from ~day 20, and books **+6.26 annualized Sharpe over 2013's 109 periods** — in a window where the benchmark *cannot trade at all*. Re-running on the apples-to-apples window:

| window | periods | gated-B1 net-of-cost | A2 best (long/flat) | family SPA K=8, net-of-cost |
| -- | -: | -: | -: | -: |
| full (first pass) | 4581 | 1.047 | 1.327 | **p = 0.034** ✓ |
| **post-gate-warmup (k ≥ 200)** | 4381 | 1.071 | 1.232 | **p = 0.057** ✗ |
| from 2014-01-01 | 4472 | 1.060 | 1.168 | **p = 0.083** ✗ |

A2's **point estimate beats gated-B1 in every window**, but the multiplicity-corrected significance **evaporates once the benchmark's handicap is removed**. The full-window p is robust to bootstrap noise (seeds 0.0285–0.0415; blocks 0.034–0.040) — so this is a **window artifact, not sampling noise**. The honest headline is the corrected p on the common window: **0.057, not significant**.

(The long/flat-only sub-family clears — K=4, p=0.017 full / 0.0245 post-warmup — but selecting it *because* the short arms lost is precisely the post-hoc cherry-pick the K=8 correction exists to prevent. It is reported, not adopted.)

### The same audit exonerates A1<a name="the-same-audit-exonerates-a1"></a>

A1's regime uses the *same* 200-day `sma_gate`, so A1 is flat during the warm-up too — **no asymmetry**. Verified: A1-long/flat's daily net-of-cost gap over gated-B1 is stable, **+0.0196 (full) → +0.0201 (post-warmup)**. iter-049's A1 net-of-cost claims are **not** warm-up-inflated. (Separately: A1-long/flat at *daily* cadence is not significant — K=2 net-of-cost SPA p=0.084; iter-049's significance came from the weekly/biweekly cadence series, a different, lower-turnover object. No contradiction.)

## Re-scoping iter-049's 2014 rejection (reports `07`, `08`)<a name="re-scoping-iter-049s-2014-rejection-reports-07-08"></a>

iter-049 rejected A1-long/flat on its 2014 worst slice and wrote that *"gated-B1's simple 200-day gate sat 2014 out — a point for gated-B1's robustness."*

**The economic claim stands.** Verified 2014:

| 2014 | exposed | mean \|pos\| | total P&L | maxDD | ann Sharpe |
| -- | -: | -: | -: | -: | -: |
| gated-B1 | 12.6 % | 0.034 | **−5.5 %** | **6.0 %** | −2.07 |
| A1-long/flat (vol 0.12) | 27.4 % | 0.264 | −8.4 % | 8.4 % | −1.80 |

gated-B1 *did* largely sit 2014 out and lost **less**, in both P&L and drawdown.

**What does not stand is the leg used to express it.** Because the leg is Sharpe-based and exposure-blind, gated-B1's 2014 Sharpe (−2.07) is *worse* than A1-long/flat's (−1.80) despite the smaller loss — so **gated-B1 would fail the leg on the very year it navigated better**. A1-long/flat's worst-slice rejection was therefore **never benchmark-relative**, and a worst-slice failure (A1's, or A2's 0/8) is **uninformative** until the leg is made benchmark-relative or drawdown-based. That is a pre-registered-protocol change → **human Phase-5 call**. `07` and `08` are re-scoped accordingly; the registry records stand (append-only audits of the *literal* bar outcome).

> An earlier draft of this report over-corrected, asserting gated-B1 "did not sit 2014 out; it lost more." That was **economically false** and is withdrawn — caught in review before merge.

## Instrument QA (before any number was read)<a name="instrument-qa-before-any-number-was-read"></a>

gated-B1 reproduces **both** frozen figures (zero-fee 1.2441 vs 1.244; net-of-cost 1.0471 vs 1.047). Reconstruct identity on **real** data: `max_k |Σ_a pos·ret − net_returns| = 1.4e-17`. Every toggle changes `net_returns`. `short="off"` has **zero** negative positions across all 4 trials × all assets × all periods (asserted); `short="on"` is short-active ~73 % of periods. All metrics finite; `var_trials` = 1.38e-5. The book itself is leak-tested with a boundary tight enough that a **1-step peek** fails it (iter-052).

## Verdict and next steps<a name="verdict-and-next-steps"></a>

- **A2 does not beat gated-B1 net-of-cost** on the corrected, apples-to-apples test (K=8, post-warmup, **p=0.057**). **gated-B1 remains the deployable bar.** An honest "no significant beat" is success.
- **Registry** (`family="A1"`, records 25…32, `notes: variant=A2-donchian`): the 4 `short="on"` arms → **`reject`** (cost-killed). The 4 `short="off"` arms → **`park`**: point-estimate net-of-cost superior in *every* window and ~2.5× cheaper turnover than A1, with the only things standing between them and a verdict being two **pre-registered-protocol questions that belong to the human**. Budget: 32/40 A-family trials; **8 held in reserve**.
- **Escalated to the human (Phase-5)**: (i) the **worst-slice leg is mis-calibrated** — it fails gated-B1 itself; make it benchmark-relative / drawdown-based, or exclude partial-year stubs; (ii) the **evaluation window** — should a challenger without a long warm-up be credited for a window where the benchmark structurally cannot trade? (iii) the standing net-of-cost-SPA refinement (`07`).
- **Autonomous follow-ups**: A2 at **4h** (its native band per §5, with a 4h gated-B1 rebuilt); a longer-cadence / breakout-hold sweep on the long/flat arms; A2's **2026 weakness** (it holds longs into the visible-2026 downtrend while gated-B1 is flat) — a real, if 89-period, caveat.
- Deployment, the holdout, and the capture pipeline were untouched.
