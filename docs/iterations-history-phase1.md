Fix (judge panel over three executed designs): **cross-stream corroboration** — a boundary-crossing event is *held* (spilled to parts at `flush_rows`) until a second witness confirms the hour: another stream, or the 5-min-handicapped wall clock (permissive only, never a veto). Witnesses are **clamped at the wall clock** so an unconfirmed stamp can never corroborate (kills both poisoning shapes), the stream witness measures the clock's *rate* not value (a constant offset cancels — a lagging clock cannot darken a quiet pair), `MAX_TS_ABSURD` (1 day) rejects coherently-wrong streams, and never-confirmed held rows spill as `.held####.parquet` — quarantined, redeemable, unable to fabricate an hour.
