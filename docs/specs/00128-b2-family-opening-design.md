## Hypothesis (trial 1, pre-registered by this spec's sha256)

A two-sigma-high trailing funding print marks crowded long positioning in the perpetual, and the spot book's long in that asset at that boundary carries more downside than the ensemble's trend signal prices: **halving the asset's position while its 30-day funding z-score exceeds +2 improves the ensemble's net-of-cost performance, through a lower drawdown at the engaged stamps, against the unconditioned ensemble.** Arms: **A** = the adopted equal-weight three-arm A2-4h ensemble exactly as `crossfreq_system.py` builds it; **B** = the same ensemble passed through the overlay. One treatment (the de-risk), judged by the pre-registered decision matrix below.

## D1 — the grid is the ensemble's own 4h boundary

The overlay acts where the ensemble decides, at the 4h close-time boundary `ts[k] + 4h` the pinned contract keys every position on (spec `00045` F3 and `expand_daily_positions`); the master plan's §6 puts funding and OI features in the 4h signal layer. The 1h grid spec `00110` D3 built stays unused by this family's trial 1: a same-hour de-risk between boundaries is an execution-layer knob, not a conditioning of the signal, and a trial on it is its own pre-registration. (Closed world: the grids spec `00110` D3 names are 1h and 4h; trial 1 takes 4h.)

## D2 — the feature is the 30-day funding z-score, as-of at the boundary close, on normalised stamps

- The asset's own 8h funding prints from `derivatives-funding` (`PERP_SYMBOLS` maps the ten EUR bases; the two /BTC legs have no perp and are outside the family, as they are outside the ensemble). Prints whose `interval_hours` ≠ 8 arrive as `None` (spec `00110` D7; SOL's 101).
- **Stamp normalisation (the first ruling T0023 records)**: a print stamped within the hour's first minute is the settlement at that hour — the stamp is truncated to the hour before alignment. The look-ahead this admits is the print's own offset, at most 0.047 s measured; a print more than 60 s past the hour is not normalised and is refused by the emission (none exists; the enumeration is the substrate, 68,281 prints, max offset 0.047 s).
- `funding_zscore(window=90)`: 90 prints = 30 days at 8h, inclusive, sample stdev, `None` while short or null-bearing (spec `00110` D7's pinned semantics, unchanged).
- **The grid handed to `align_asof` is the close stamp (the second ruling)**: `ts[k] + 4h` for return index `k`, never the bar-open `ts[k]` (one bar stale) and never the next bar's close (a 4h look-ahead); a print at the boundary's own hour is included (`source_ts <= grid_ts`), since the settlement is known at that instant. The emission is leak-free by `align_asof`'s contract and its truncating-prefix property test, which truncates the inputs — the prefix's bars and only the prints whose normalised stamp is at or before the prefix's last close, that close computed by the test itself — because a grid-only truncation holds under a look-ahead too.
- Emitted as `binperp_funding_z30d` per asset on the union's return index (spec `00110` D8's prefix); the record carries its coverage as `binperp_funding_z30d_coverage` (D7).

## D3 — the treatment is a fixed half-scale while the z-score exceeds +2, per asset, no hold-through

- At return index `k`, for asset `a`: if `binperp_funding_z30d[a][k]` is not `None` and `> 2.0`, arm B's position is `0.5 × arm A's position`; otherwise arm A's position unchanged. The scale is applied to the ensemble's equal-weight per-asset position after the arms' own vol targeting, as B1's scaler was.
- The threshold 2.0 and the scale 0.5 are pre-registered constants, not tuned: 2.0 is the two-sigma reading the hypothesis names, measured to engage on 4.5 % of long stamps; 0.5 is the de-risk fraction B1's vol scaler fixed (spec `00045` 4), kept so the two families' knobs compare. Changing either after a result is a new trial.
- No hold-through: the de-risk is a trade and the re-risk is a trade, each charged at 0.006/side on the position change, so the treatment pays its own turnover and the matrix sees it net of cost. (B1's hold-through was its turnover-control mechanism; this family's claim is drawdown, and a forced hold would conflate the two.)
- The ensemble is long-only (`short="off"`), so the symmetric rule for shorts at z < −2 is inert on this book and is not part of trial 1; the measured 1,379 long stamps at z < −2 are a later trial's candidate (a re-risk or the contrarian re-entry §5 names), not a treatment here.
- A `None` z-score (warm-up, SOL's dark stretch, AVAX before its first print) leaves the position unchanged: the overlay never acts on a missing feature.

## D6 — the kill bar and the decision matrix, fixed before the run

- Arm B against the frozen benchmark: `a1_kill_bar(book, benchmark_4h, n_trials=1, var_trials=0.0, mean_block=30, seed=42, n_resamples=2000, cost_stressed(×1.5), calendar-year slices keyed by each bar's close year with the window's partial years 2020 and 2026 excluded, decisive_start=1380)`; the SPA robustness grid blocks {30, 102} × seeds {42, 7, 1234} on the decisive window; the DSR leg non-binding at n_trials = 1 (expected-max-Sharpe is 0 at n = 1, so DSR ≈ PSR against 0 — the burden sits on SPA and the worst slice; stated in the record's notes, as spec `00045` F6 did).
- Head-to-head: `net_of_cost_verdict(B, A, mean_block=30, seed=42, n_resamples=2000)` on the full window and from 2021-01-01 — from the first return index whose close stamp `ts[k] + 4h` is at or after 2021-01-01T00:00Z — the first full calendar year (the from-2016 read of B1 has no analogue; the second read guards the first months, where the ensemble's lookbacks and the z-score are still warming).
- Engagement proof, per year (keyed by the close stamp's year), all four in the record: engaged stamps (asset-stamps long with z > 2), return indices where the arms' positions differ, arm B's turnover against arm A's, arm B's maximum drawdown against arm A's. Before the run, the engaged stamps and the feature's coverage are read from the subject's inputs half, which computes no return of arm B, on the data root the run reads, and must reproduce the measured basis exactly — its per-asset, per-year and coverage counts, and the inputs' block hash, which the registered record's `dataset_hash` then equals; a mismatch is an instrument finding, no run and no verdict, and a dead knob (zero engaged stamps in every year) is one. (The basis measures engagement in every year of the window; the proof is the run's own.)
- The matrix:

| Kill bar (B vs frozen benchmark) | Head-to-head (B vs A, the from-2021 read primary) | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| pass | B beats A (mean outperformance > 0 and SPA p < 0.05) and B's maximum drawdown below A's | **adopt** the overlay |
| pass | B > A point estimate, SPA n.s., and B's maximum drawdown below A's | **park** (variant candidates: threshold 2.5, scale 0.25, the z < −2 re-risk) |
| pass | B ≤ A (mean outperformance ≤ 0), or B's maximum drawdown not below A's | **reject** the overlay (B passing the bar inherits A's edge; the de-risk did not do what it claims) |
| fail | any | **reject** (a conditioning that breaks the adopted arm's bar is harmful) |

Every quantity the head-to-head column reads — mean outperformance, SPA p, maximum drawdown — is the from-2021 read's, the full-window read recorded beside it; the rows are disjoint and cover every outcome. B's maximum drawdown is *below* A's when `max_drawdown_reduction_pct` exceeds 1e-6, and *not below* otherwise: B's returns differ from A's only around engaged stamps, so when none falls inside A's worst episode B's drawdown is mathematically at or above A's and the two equity products differ by rounding alone — at most a 3.6e-12 % reduction, read in 177 of 400 synthetic books of the from-2021 read's 12,040 returns with no engaged stamp in A's worst episode (A's returns Gaussian with mean 0.0002 and standard deviation 0.004, drawn by `random.Random(seed)` for seeds 0–399; B halves 300 of them sampled from outside A's worst peak-to-trough episode; both drawdowns through `cli.validation.max_drawdown`).

## D8 — the emission module and the overlay module

- `cli/alpha/b2.py` (mirroring `b1.py`): `B2Config(z_threshold: float = 2.0, derisk_scale: float = 0.5, z_window: int = 90)` validated at construction — D2's 60 s tolerance is a ruling, a module constant, not a field; `close_grid(union_ts) -> list[datetime]` (`ts[k] + 4h` for every return index); `funding_grid_feature(prints_ts, prints_rate, prints_interval_hours, union_ts, *, config) -> list[float | None]` (the emission: normalise stamps, map off-cadence prints to `None`, z-score, as-of align onto `close_grid(union_ts)` — D2's two rulings live here and nowhere else); `derisk_scales(z_by_asset, *, config) -> dict[str, list[float]]` (1.0 or 0.5 per asset per index; `None` → 1.0); `scale_positions(asset_positions, scales) -> dict[str, list[float]]`; `net_of_cost(asset_positions, asset_returns, *, cost_per_side) -> list[float]` (D5's costing); engagement helpers keyed by the close stamp's year — `per_year_engaged_counts(asset_positions, scales, close_ts)` (asset-stamps long with a scale below 1), `per_year_positions_differ`, `per_year_turnover`, `per_year_max_drawdown` — and `book_turnover` reused from `b1.py` for the window totals.
- `coverage_by_year` (spec `00110` D6) of the feature per asset is read before the run with the engaged stamps (D6) and recorded as `binperp_funding_z30d_coverage`, numbers alone (the registry takes no stamp), so the trial cannot run blind over SOL's dark stretch or AVAX's late start.
- Known-answer tests: a planted two-sigma print scales exactly the stamps from its settlement hour to the next print's; a print 30 ms past the hour and one on the hour produce the same alignment; a print 61 s past the hour is refused; a print settled at hour H lands at the return index whose close is H — not one later (a bar-open grid) nor one earlier (the next bar's close); the truncating-prefix property of the emitted feature over truncated inputs (D2); `None` leaves the position unchanged, and so do a z of exactly +2 and a z below −2; the scale's turnover is charged (`net_of_cost` on a hand-computed two-asset case); a flat stamp at z > 2 is not an engaged stamp.

## D9 — what the trial does not decide, and what a verdict changes

- A verdict of **adopt** does not touch the live book: the substrate is backtest-only (spec `00110` D9), and a live funding feed is a new spec with its own API path; the adopted overlay is a research-registry fact until then, as spec `00045`'s was.
- A verdict of **park** names its variant candidates in the record; each is a new trial of the budget.
- A verdict of **reject** archives the hypothesis in the record and the decisions log; the family stays open for its OI and liquidation hypotheses.
