# The reading of the engine's execution cost: its maker-first ladder replayed at the deployable's cycle boundaries over the captured book

Iteration `iter-199`, topic [T0214](../open-topics/T0214-cost-basis-re-priced-at-rung-2.md), beside [T0122](../open-topics/T0122-ongoing-register-kill-criteria.md). A design in two parts, both pre-registered by this spec's sha256 before either exists: a frozen derived dataset — the engine's order ladder replayed against the captured depth-100 book and trades at the deployable's cycle boundaries, each order posted as a cycle plan's first leg — and a no-slot reading over it of what a side costs, against the registered 0.006. No hypothesis, no bar, no verdict, no slot. Serials: iter-199 and 00153 are minted past iter-198 and 00152, which the branch `research/iter-198-deployable-revalidation` holds unmerged; that branch merges first.

## The measured basis

Measured 2026-10-29 on `zcrypto-ops`, the workstation, by the controller.

- **The engine's `execute` intent** (`cli/engine/executor.py`): `_limit_price` joins the touch — a buy at the bid, a sell at the ask — as a post-only GTC order; `_reprice` runs only on the venue's post-only rejection or its accept-then-cancel ("Two callers: the venue's synchronous post-only rejection and its accept-then-cancel"), never on a touch that moves away, so the order rests at its first price; `_TIME_BOX` is 15 minutes, after which `_fallback` sends at most `_MAX_IOC_ATTEMPTS` 3 limit-IOC orders "at the opposite touch, each sized to what is still owed", each bounded by `_ACK_WAIT` 30 s, and a remainder after the third ends `unfilled`.
- **The C1 fill model** (`cli/panel/fills.py`, spec 00151 D2) posts every 15 s at the touch and cancels an order once its side's best quote stands a tick away for 5 s (`tick_cancel`, `CANCEL_AFTER_S` 5): its fill rate within 900 s is a floor on the engine's unchased order's, never the engine's own. Reading 8's unconditioned fill rates, 0.25 %–16.6 % of posts within 900 s, are that floor. Its fill rule — `apply_trade`, the queue-ahead cap in `tick_cancel`, `post_orders`'s sizes at the touch — is the one this reading reuses unchanged.
- **Rung 2's own fills** (`docs/reference/rung2-box/days.md`): entry day 2026-10-05's counters "filled 9 … fills maker 10, taker 0"; to 2026-10-25 (the 2026-W43 read), 25 fills, "maker 96.3 % / taker 3.7 % of the priceable book", its two taker fills each an order that rested unfilled through its 15-minute box and filled by the IOC after it (2026-10-10 DOT/EUR, 2026-10-22 BTC/EUR) — far too few to price a blend; T0214's trigger asks for 30.
- **The fee schedule and the registered basis**: tier-1 maker 0.0040 a side and taker 0.0080 (master plan §2); the registered 0.006 a side is `fee_per_side` 0.0040 plus `spread_per_side` 0.0020 (`cli/research/subjects.py`'s `_RECORD44_CONFIG`).
- **When the deployable trades**: at its cycle boundaries, 00, 04, 08, 12, 16 and 20Z; the 12Z cycle's record was journaled at 12:01:42Z–12:02:05Z on each of the 19 rung-2 box days the record stamps to the second (`docs/reference/rung2-box/days.md` on `records/rung2-week-4`: 10 days at 12:01:42Z, 8 at 12:01:43Z, 1 at 12:02:05Z), and within 12:01Z on three more. The engine drafts a cycle's plan on its next tick after the record (`on_boundary`, called from `cli/engine/node.py` once the cycle has journaled), holds every placed leg in that one plan, sells first (`assemble_plans`), and runs the legs one at a time (`_pump`, `cli/engine/executor.py`), each up to its time box and its IOC attempts — so only the plan's first leg posts close to the record, and a later leg minutes after it.
- **The seed**: the panel tree `/mnt/zhao-crypto/l2-panel` holds each hour's `<HH>.state.json`, the end-of-hour book `seed_book` loads (`cli/panel/fills.py`); the controller's read of the state of the hour before each of the span's 6,192 pair-boundaries (`_state_path`, `load_state`) found 0 absent and 0 holding no book.
- **The replay's cost**: a full captured hour of BTC/EUR in 6.6 s and of ADA/EUR in 5.5 s (spec 00151's measured basis); a ladder episode needs one boundary hour's first 1,020 s, so the span's 6,192 pair-boundaries (6 a day × 86 days × 12 pairs) cost at most about 11.4 CPU-hours at the full-hour rate, timed after the hash.

## Operating model

- **One machine, one controller.** The dataset is produced on `zcrypto-ops`, which mounts the NAS read-only, by the controller alone; it writes a new root `data/l2-ladder-<stamp>/` in the main checkout — a derivation to a new path, never a write into an existing set. The reading runs in the branch's worktree over a symlink to that frozen root. No dispatch reads `/mnt/zhao-crypto/`.
- **The processes.** One `uv run zcrypto panel ladder …` process per pair, each awaited to its exit, their outputs disjoint by pair; one `uv run zcrypto research eval --reading` process; no daemon, timer or concurrent writer of one file; the capture pipeline is not touched.
- **No trial-attempts log**: a reading spends no trial.

## The question (no hypothesis, no bar)

When the engine's maker-first ladder places a €100 or €1,000 order on a pair as a cycle plan's first leg — posted at the touch two minutes after the boundary, resting unchased for 15 minutes, then crossing at the opposite touch — how often does it fill as a maker, per pair and side, and what does a side cost against the mid at the post, per pair and side and over the ten EUR legs, against the registered 0.006?

## D2 — the instrument: the ladder replay as a frozen derived dataset

- **The producer**, `zcrypto panel ladder <primary-root> <out-root> --reconciled-root <overlay> --panel-root <panel> --pair <PAIR> --since <hour> --until <hour>` (`--until` exclusive), new, in `cli/panel/ladder.py`, reusing `cli/panel/fills.py`'s `_post` (with `post_orders` inside it), `apply_trade`, `seed_book`, its hour reads, its production mark `_mark`, and its day writer, run record and second listing, which take the ladder's record name and boundary hours as keywords whose defaults keep C1's behaviour; the queue cap, two lines inside `tick_cancel`, is extracted into a helper `cap_queue` that `tick_cancel` and the ladder both call — a refactor that changes neither C1's behaviour, which its tests hold, nor its dataset. Like the fill producer, the ladder producer marks its out root in production and refuses an out root holding its `manifest.json`.
- **An episode** per pair at each cycle boundary `B` of the span: the book seeded from the panel's state of the hour before `B` (`seed_book`) — with no state, from the hour's opening snapshot, an hour opening with an update counted unanchored; a state the read cannot open or that holds no book refuses the pair's run — hour `B`'s book messages and trades replayed second by second as `simulate_hour` replays them; at `B + 120 s` four orders posted at the touch through `_post` — two sides, the C1 rungs €100 and €1,000 of the quote, each joining the back of the queue — skipped and counted as `_post` skips and counts them (a book stale past 30 s, or one-sided). **Author's call — `B + 120 s`, every pair's orders posted as a plan's first leg**: the engine drafts its plan on the first tick after the cycle's record (about `B + 102 s`) and posts the first leg at that leg's first quote; `B + 120 s` approximates that instant and is never a measured one, and a later leg's instant is left unmeasured (Out of scope).
- **The fill rule**: spec 00151 D2's, unchanged — `apply_trade` for every print up to each second, the queue ahead capped at the level's resting quantity as the second before left it (`cap_queue`) — **with no away-cancel**: an order rests at its first price until it fills or until `post + 900 s`, the time box.
- **The fallback leg**: every order, filled or not, is recorded with the opposite touch at `post + 900 s` — its price and resting quantity — and the mid at that second, none of the three where the book is stale or one-sided there; an order unfilled at that second falls back, priced at that touch, the first IOC's price. An order larger than the touch's quantity is recorded as larger; the later IOC attempts at deeper levels are not modelled, so such an order's fallback cost is a floor. The fill rule fills an order whole or not at all, so an order the prints partly met in the box is recorded unfilled and priced whole at the fallback, where the engine pays the maker's cost on the met part and sends the IOC for the remainder alone — a departure the replay does not size.
- **A row** per order: `ts` (the post), `pair`, `side` (`bid` a buy, `ask` a sell), `size` (rung 0 or 1), `price`, `our_qty`, `queue_ahead_qty`, `mid_at_post`, `spread_at_post_bps`, `filled`, `time_to_fill_s`, `fill_price`, `box_end_mid`, `box_end_touch_price`, `box_end_touch_qty`.
- **The layout**: `data/l2-ladder-<stamp>/<BASE>/<QUOTE>/<YYYY>/<MM>/<DD>.parquet` + `.sha256` (the frame's `dataset_hash`), one day per file, a run record per pair and a conformant `manifest.json`, in the forms spec 00151 D2 gives `l2-fills-<stamp>`, each day's record and the manifest's second listing over the boundary hours alone.
- **The span and pairs**: spec 00151 D2's, 2026-08-01T00:00Z ≤ `B` < 2026-10-26T00:00Z, the twelve pairs the capture holds; a boundary hour whose book hour the capture lacks is a skipped episode, counted, and one whose trades hour it lacks is replayed with no print and listed — a trades hour with no print writes no file, and spec 00151's measured basis names two such quiet boundary hours, AVAX/EUR's 2026-08-30 00Z and SOL/BTC's 2026-10-17 04Z.
- **Proven on known answers before it runs on the real set**: a synthetic hour whose prints fill the bid at its 300th second and never the ask; an order the touch moves away from and back to, filled by a later print — the case C1's rule cancels; an order unfilled at the box end, its opposite touch and quantity read at exactly `post + 900 s`; an order larger than that touch's quantity recorded as larger; a box end stale or one-sided, no touch recorded; a boundary hour without its trades hour replayed with no print; a stale book skipped and counted; an episode seeded from a planted state sidecar.

## D3 — the reading

- **Per pair × side × size**, over the span and per month window (2026-08, 2026-09, 2026-10 through the 25th): the posts; the maker share, filled by `post + 900 s`, over the posts; the median time to fill; and the cost of a side against `mid_at_post`, signed against the order, in return units: a maker fill `s·(fill_price − mid_at_post)/mid_at_post + 0.0040`, a fallback `s·(box_end_touch_price − mid_at_post)/mid_at_post + 0.0080`, `s` +1 for a buy and −1 for a sell; the mean cost over the priced posts — a fallback with no touch recorded is counted unpriced, outside the mean — and its 95 % interval from `cli/validation/bootstrap.py`'s `bootstrap_ci` over those posts in boundary order, mean block 6 (one day), 2,000 resamples, seed 42; the share of the priced fallbacks larger than their touch.
- **No separate adverse-selection figure**, which the owner's item 2 names (D1): against `mid_at_post` a side's cost is its whole shortfall from the same position taken at that mid — once an order fills, the position moves as that one would, and an order the price leaves pays the move at the fallback — so the mean over the priced posts already carries the selection of which orders fill.
- **The blend**: per size, at each boundary the mean over the ten EUR legs of each leg's mean cost over its priced sides — each leg weighted equally, a leg with no priced side at that boundary left out, and a boundary with no priced leg left out of the series; the blend is that per-boundary series' mean, its interval by the same bootstrap over the series in boundary order.
- **Read against the registered 0.006 a side, never a verdict**: the reading prices; re-pricing the registered basis is T0214's decision, the owner's, as a new registry record.

## D4 — the hash rule and the order of work

After the hash: the timed episodes, the production, its manifest and catalog entry, the reading's run, its entry; T0214's and T0122's findings.

## D5 — what the reading changes

- Nothing on the live path. Its figures go to T0214 beside rung 2's measured point, for the owner's re-pricing decision, and to T0122 as the fees-and-slippage part of the execution error the paired live-minus-simulation harness will read against; measured from the mid at the post, it leaves out the drift from `B` to the post, part of that error's timing.

## Out of scope

Changing the engine's ladder or its time box; re-pricing the registered cost basis (T0214's decision); the IOC attempts past the first touch; a later leg's post instant — the engine runs a plan's legs one at a time, sells first, each up to its time box and its IOC attempts, so a later leg posts minutes after the first, in an order the plan's composition decides cycle by cycle; the side the deployable trades — its plan places at most one side of a leg at a boundary, the one the leg's target less its holding gives (`decide_leg`, `cli/engine/draftplan.py`), where the replay posts both sides at every boundary, so the selection it reads is unconditional on the deployable's signal; the engine's partial maker fill; the engine's revoke of a resting order after `_QUOTE_SILENCE`, 30 s with no quote on its own feed, which ends the leg with no fallback and refuses the plan's later legs — the replay reads the capture's book, not that feed, and rests the order through a stall; sizes below €100 — at one post, a smaller order fills whenever a larger one does, since a fill needs the prints to meet the queue ahead and the order, so the €100 maker share bounds a smaller order's from below; any change to `cli/panel/fills.py`'s behaviour, the capture pipeline, the panel materializer or an existing dataset.
