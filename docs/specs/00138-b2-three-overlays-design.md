- **The contrast.** `event_contrast(flags, forwards, mean_block=24, n_resamples=2000, seed=42)`, new in `cli/alpha/b2.py`: per forward series, per coin with at least two events and two others whose forward is defined, and pooled over those coins, `[events, others, mean after an event, mean after the others, their difference, its 2.5 % and 97.5 % bounds]`, the bounds the percentiles of the difference over `stationary_bootstrap_indices(n_hours, mean_block=24, n_resamples=2000, seed=42)` — one draw of hour indices shared by every series and coin, so the pooled bounds keep the coins' common hours together; a resample holding no event or no other for a row is skipped for that row.
- **The 12 keys**: `fwd_logret_1h`, `fwd_logret_4h`, `fwd_logret_24h`, `fwd_rv_1h`, `fwd_rv_4h`, `fwd_rv_24h`, each the contrast's rows by coin and `pooled`; `events_per_asset` and `zscore_hours_per_asset`; and the constants `threshold` 2.0, `zscore_hours` 720, `mean_block` 24 and `n_resamples` 2000.
