## The question (no hypothesis, no bar)

Master plan §5's C1: short-term reversal at 15m–1h, "only testable as maker-only with queue-position realism from our own captured L2 data". The question in two halves: **(a) when a passive order of €100 or €1,000 joins the back of the queue at the best bid or ask, how often does it fill within 1, 5 and 15 minutes, and what does the mid do after it fills (the adverse selection); (b) conditioned on a 15m move of the mid beyond one or two of its trailing daily scales, does a passive order posted against the move — filled under (a)'s model — earn, over the next hour, a gross return (the mid's move in its favour plus the half-spread it captured) above the 0.0080 maker round trip, in every complete month and over the span?** A cell clearing the potential criterion (D5) names the trial a later spec would pre-register; none clearing it is the evidence the owner's C1 family call reads.

## D2 — the instrument: the fill model as a frozen derived dataset

- **The producer**, `zcrypto panel fills <primary-root> <out-root> --reconciled-root <overlay> --pair <PAIR> --until <stamp>`, new, in `cli/panel/fills.py` beside the materializer, reading the canonical book and the healed trades reconciled-first, hour by hour in `canonical_segments` order, the book threaded across hours as `materialize_hour` threads it. **Author's call — one producer run per pair, the controller's, on the ops host**; a run is resumable by the hours already written (a per-pair watermark as the panel's), so a stop costs nothing.
- **The posting grid**: every 15 seconds on the hour's grid (240 posts an hour), on both sides, at two sizes — the notionals €100 and €1,000 of the pair's quote (`notionals_for(quote)`'s first two rungs, so a BTC-quoted pair posts the BTC quantity worth those amounts at the panel's pinned FX reference) — a passive order at the best bid (a buy) or best ask (a sell) **joining the back of the queue**: `queue_ahead_qty` = the level's resting quantity at the post. **Author's call — the back of the queue, no cancellation ahead credited**: the capture carries level quantities, never order identities, so cancellations ahead of us are invisible; crediting none is the conservative bound, and a fill model that overstates fills would license a family the venue kills. Posts with no best quote on that side (an empty side, a stale book: `stale_seconds > 30` as the panel's caveat) are skipped, counted.
- **The fill rule**: the order fills when the traded quantity at its price on the opposite aggressor side (a sell trade at or below our bid price fills a bid; a buy trade at or above our ask fills an ask), cumulated from the post, reaches `queue_ahead_qty + our_qty`; partially filled quantity is not credited (full fill or none). The order is **cancelled** when the best quote on its side moves away from its price by one tick or more for 5 consecutive seconds — the price moved off and a maker who stays is adversely selected by construction — or at the horizon **900 s**. Each post records `filled` (bool), `time_to_fill_s`, `cancelled_s`, `fill_price`, `mid_at_post`, `spread_at_post_bps`, `queue_ahead_qty`, `queue_ahead_notional`, `mid_at_fill_plus_60s`, `mid_at_fill_plus_900s`, `mid_at_post_plus_900s`, `mid_at_post_plus_3600s`, `stale_seconds_at_post`, the pair, side, size and `ts`. **Author's call — horizons 60, 300 and 900 s** read off `time_to_fill_s` at the reading, one dataset serving all three.
- **The layout**: `data/l2-fills-<stamp>/<BASE>/<QUOTE>/<YYYY>/<MM>/<DD>.parquet` + `.sha256`, one day per file, ~23,040 rows a day per pair (240 × 2 × 2 × 24); a `manifest.json` with the span, the parameters (grid 15 s, sizes, cancel rule, horizon), the producer's code ref and the per-file hashes, in the form `ohlc-15m`'s manifest takes; the span **2026-07-08T13:00Z → 2026-10-27T23:59Z**, frozen — hours the capture lacks are honest gaps, recorded in the manifest. The dataset is cataloged in `docs/reference/data-catalog-full.md` in the same change (`iteration-closeout`'s rule).
- **The pairs**: the twelve the capture holds — the ten EUR legs and `ETH/BTC`, `SOL/BTC`.
- **Proven on known answers before it runs on the real set**: a synthetic hour whose trades are written to fill exactly the third post and no other; a cancellation at the sixth second after the level moves; a stale book skipped; the full-fill rule refusing a partial; the adverse-selection read at +60 s and +900 s against planted mids; an hour opening with an update and no carried book refused as the panel refuses it.

## D4 — the reading's computes half: the fill table and the reversal surface

- **The fill table**, per pair × side × size × horizon `H ∈ {60, 300, 900}` s, per calendar month and over the span: the fill rate (`filled` with `time_to_fill_s ≤ H` over posts), the median time to fill of the filled, and the adverse selection — the mean of the mid's move from `fill_price` to `mid_at_fill_plus_60s` and to `+900s`, signed against the order (a bid's fill followed by a lower mid is positive adverse selection), in bps.
- **The reversal surface**: the mid at the posts gives a 15m series per pair (the post at each quarter-hour's boundary); its log return `r[t]` and trailing 96-bar scale σ (one day of 15m bars) as spec 00150 D3 defines them; at a bar whose `|r| > θ·σ`, `θ ∈ {1, 2}`, the trade is the passive order posted **against the move** at the bar's close — a bid after a down move, an ask after an up move — at size `S ∈ {€100, €1k}`, filled or not under the dataset's own rows (the post at that boundary, that side, that size), horizon 900 s. A **cell** is `(pair, θ, S, side)`: 12 × 2 × 2 × 2 = **96 cells**. Its reads: the fill rate among its signal bars; among the filled, the **gross return per filled trade** = the mid's move from `fill_price` to `mid_at_post_plus_3600s` in the trade's favour plus the half-spread captured (`spread_at_post_bps / 2`), in return units; the **net** = gross − **0.0080**; per complete month and over the span, `n`, the means, and the bootstrap `p` of the mean net being at or below zero, `reality_check_pvalue([[g_k − 0.0080] for each filled trade k], mean_block=30, n_resamples=4000, seed=42)["p_value"]` (the floor 1/4001 under the Bonferroni bound 0.05/96 = 0.00052…).
- **Author's call — the hurdle is the maker round trip 0.0080, the spread credited**: a maker earns the half-spread and pays the fee; a taker's 0.016 is not the family's premise. **Author's call — the unfilled signal bars count as no trade**, not as a taker fill: the family is maker-only by its own definition, and a cell's fill rate is reported beside its return so a thin fill rate is read for what it is.

## D5 — the potential criterion, pre-registered

- **The floor**: a cell has at least 100 filled trades in each complete calendar month of the span (August, September, October 2026) — a thinner cell is unread.
- **Potential** needs all four: (1) its fill rate at 900 s is at least 0.50 over the span; (2) its mean gross return per filled trade exceeds 0.0080 in each complete month; (3) over the span; (4) its bootstrap p is below 0.05/96. A cell with potential names trial 1: that rule, the fill model as this dataset computes it, judged by the family's kill bar against the frozen 4h benchmark and a head-to-head against the adopted ensemble, a maker-fee cost model stated in that spec. None with potential is the evidence the owner's C1 family call reads; the reading closes nothing. The fill table (D4) is read beside the criterion for what it says about maker-only execution in general — a finding for every family's execution scheduler, no criterion of this reading.
- **The contamination**: the reversal surface is a search over 96 cells on the whole span; a trial it names is designed on this data, counts C1's true trials with these 96 looks in its design, and its spec says so. The holdout is untouched: the capture starts 2026-07-08, the holdout window ends 2026-07-09 — the first 36 hours of the capture overlap the window's last day; **the dataset's span starts 2026-07-10T00:00Z**, so no row of it lies inside the holdout window (an amendment of D2's span start, made here before any production).
