## Operating model

- **Two machines, one controller.** The dataset is produced on `zcrypto-ops`, the workstation that mounts the NAS read-only, by the controller alone, as `cli/panel`'s materializer is run; it writes to a new root `data/l2-fills-<stamp>/` in the main checkout — a derivation to a new path, never a write into an existing set (`data/` canonical sets are immutable). The reading runs in the branch's worktree over a symlink to that frozen root. No dispatch reads `/mnt/zhao-crypto/`.
- **The processes.** The production is one `uv run zcrypto panel fills …` process per pair, each awaited to its exit, their outputs disjoint by pair; the reading is one `uv run zcrypto research eval` process; no daemon, timer or concurrent writer of one file; the live capture pipeline is not touched — the producer reads the archive, never the daemon's output directory.
- **No trial-attempts log.** A subject with no rule registers no trial: `--reading` keeps the run on `docs/reference/reading-ledger.jsonl`, `attempt` null; C's 10 slots untouched.

## The question (no hypothesis, no bar)

Master plan §5's C1: short-term reversal at 15m–1h, "only testable as maker-only with queue-position realism from our own captured L2 data". The question in two halves: **(a) when a passive order of €100 or €1,000 joins the back of the queue at the best bid or ask, how often does it fill within 1, 5 and 15 minutes, and what does the mid do after it fills (the adverse selection); (b) conditioned on a 15m move of the mid beyond one or two of its trailing daily scales, does a passive order posted against the move — filled under (a)'s model — earn, over the next hour, a gross return (the move from its fill price to the mid in its favour, which carries the half-spread it captured) above the 0.0080 maker round trip, in each of the span's three month windows and over the span?** A cell clearing the potential criterion (D5) names the trial a later spec would pre-register; none clearing it is the evidence the owner's C1 family call reads.

## D2 — the instrument: the fill model as a frozen derived dataset

- **The producer**, `zcrypto panel fills <primary-root> <out-root> --reconciled-root <overlay> --panel-root <panel> --pair <PAIR> --since <hour> --until <hour>` (`--until` exclusive), new, in `cli/panel/fills.py` beside the materializer, reading the canonical book and the healed trades reconciled-first, hour by hour in `canonical_segments` order, the book threaded across hours as `materialize_hour` threads it and seeded at a run's first hour — the span's or a resume's — and at the hour after a gap from the panel's state sidecar of the hour before (`load_state`); without that sidecar the hours up to the first snapshot-opening hour are skipped and counted, and a sidecar present but unreadable refuses the run — a read error is never a counted gap. Each kind's listing — the book's and the trades' — is recorded per pair, every hour of the run's `[since, until)` with the root it was listed from, the overlay or the primary, or as absent, and listed once more through `canonical_segments` before the manifest is written: an hour that second listing holds from another root than the record names — absent at the run, or listed from the primary where the second listing reads the overlay — refuses the set, a hole of the soft mount's listing (a directory scan's error is swallowed, and an overlay root that cannot be stat'ed reads as no overlay) and never a gap; a root the run or the manifest reads through that cannot be read as a directory refuses it, the root named. The premise is an archive no writer changes between the two listings: the reconciler re-scans its trailing 48 settled hours each cycle, which hold no span hour from 2026-10-28T01:12Z (its NAS replica a pull cycle behind), and the trade backfill's daily pass, over the whole archive with no window, mints into the overlay each hour whose rows repeat a trade id and each hour a print it recovers from a trade-id gap falls in, while the span's trades hold no repeat and no gap, every hour they lack quiet (the measured basis) — so no span hour is left for either writer to heal. The compare is not what rules a re-mint out: one changes a primary-listed hour's root, a refusal, but only the rows of an hour the overlay already holds, which both listings read from the same root. An hour absent from both listings is a gap the manifest records: the measured basis names the span's, no book hour and the fifteen quiet trades hours, and a set whose gaps differ is not read (D6's basis case); a healed hour both listings read from its primary — its overlay directory unscannable at each — passes the compare too, the residual the same case catches by each pair's hours listed from the overlay, by kind, held against the overlay's own count taken apart from both listings. **Author's call — one producer run per pair, the controller's, on the ops host**; a run is resumable by the days already written (a per-pair watermark as the panel's), so a stop costs nothing; a per-pair run record holds the run's parameters and code ref, and a resume under other ones is refused — a change is a new stamp.
- **The posting grid**: every 15 seconds on the hour's grid (240 posts an hour), on both sides, at two sizes — the notionals €100 and €1,000 of the pair's quote (`notionals_for(quote)`'s first two rungs, so a BTC-quoted pair posts the BTC quantity worth those amounts at the panel's pinned FX reference) — a passive order at the best bid (a buy) or best ask (a sell) **joining the back of the queue**: `queue_ahead_qty` = the level's resting quantity at the post. **Author's call — the back of the queue, no cancellation ahead credited past what the level shows**: the capture carries level quantities, never order identities, so cancellations ahead of us are invisible and none is credited; but every order ahead of ours rests in the captured level at our price, so the queue ahead is never more than that level's quantity — the fill rule's bound, an upper bound the capture proves, crediting no cancellation it does not show. A fill model that overstates fills would license a family the venue kills, and one that holds a queue the capture shows gone drops the fills that follow it, the adverse ones. Posts at a book with either side empty (no mid to record) or stale (`stale_seconds > 30`, the panel's caveat) are skipped, counted in orders.
- **The fill rule**: the trades on the opposite aggressor side at its price (a sell trade at our bid's price meets a bid; a buy trade at our ask's price an ask) meet the queue ahead first and then the order, which fills once they have met `our_qty` of it — with the queue ahead moved by them alone, once their quantity cumulated from the post reaches `queue_ahead_qty + our_qty`; the queue ahead is `queue_ahead_qty` at the post, less those trades, and at each second never more than the captured level's resting quantity at its price as it stood a second before — zero once the level is gone, read after that second's trades, and never raised by a level refilled behind the order, whose refilling orders joined after ours; the level is read a second late because the measured basis does not rule out the book update a print causes landing a second before that print, where the level read at each second's own boundary would count that print twice against the queue ahead — so a trade that empties the level meets the queue ahead alone whichever of the two seconds its update lands at, and the late read errs only toward fewer fills; the trades through its price (a sell below our bid, a buy above our ask) meet the order with the queue ahead already met, every order resting at its price coming before them, so their whole quantity adds to what the trades have met of the order, which fills once that reaches `our_qty`; partially filled quantity is not credited (full fill or none), a trade through the price short of the order's size among it. Prices are compared as `Decimal`, the book's own type — the fill rule's price test, the tick-away test and the tick, the smallest positive gap between levels — and written as Float64. The order is **cancelled** when the best quote on its side moves away from its price by one tick or more for 5 consecutive seconds — the price moved off and a maker who stays is adversely selected by construction — or at the horizon **900 s**. Each post records `filled` (bool), `time_to_fill_s`, `cancelled_s`, `fill_price`, `mid_at_post`, `spread_at_post_bps`, `queue_ahead_qty`, `queue_ahead_notional`, `mid_at_fill_plus_60s`, `mid_at_fill_plus_900s`, `mid_at_post_plus_900s`, `mid_at_post_plus_3600s`, `stale_seconds_at_post`, the pair, side, size and `ts`; a post's row is complete once its `post + 3600 s` mid is read — each mid the book's at the first second at or after its instant with both sides quoted and the book fresh (`stale_seconds` at most 30, or unknown, the posts' own test: a frozen book's mid is a carried one) — and a mid the data does not reach (the span's end, a gap) is null, never a carried mid, a post still open when the data ends censored (`filled` false, `cancelled_s` null). **Author's call — horizons 60, 300 and 900 s** read off `time_to_fill_s` at the reading, one dataset serving all three.
- **The layout**: `data/l2-fills-<stamp>/<BASE>/<QUOTE>/<YYYY>/<MM>/<DD>.parquet` + `.sha256`, one day per file, a row filed under its post's UTC day and a day's file written once every order posted that day is complete, ~23,040 rows a day per pair (240 × 2 × 2 × 24); a `manifest.json` with the span, the parameters (grid 15 s, sizes, cancel rule, horizon), the producer's code ref, each pair's per-day counts — posts, skips, hours unanchored and absent, trades hours absent, the hours each kind listed from the overlay — and the per-file hashes, in the form `ohlc-15m`'s manifest takes; the span **2026-08-01T00:00Z ≤ ts < 2026-10-26T00:00Z**, frozen — the three month windows D5's floor reads, August and September whole and October through the 25th (25 of its 31 days, partial, so named wherever the three are listed), which also keeps every row clear of the holdout window (its last day is 2026-07-09); it ends with 2026-10-25, the reconciler's window past it, no trades hour in it for the backfill to re-mint and every one it lacks quiet (the producer, above); hours the capture lacks are honest gaps, recorded in the manifest. The dataset is cataloged in `docs/reference/data-catalog-full.md` in the same change (`iteration-closeout`'s rule).
- **The pairs**: the twelve the capture holds — the ten EUR legs and `ETH/BTC`, `SOL/BTC`.
- **Proven on known answers before it runs on the real set**: a synthetic hour whose trades are written to fill exactly the third post and no other; a cancellation at the sixth second after the level moves; a stale book skipped; the full-fill rule refusing a partial, at the price and through it; a trade through the price of the order's size filling it with the queue ahead standing; a level emptied with no trade and then a trade through the price of the order's size filling it before its cancel; a level cut by an update capping the queue ahead, a level refilled never raising it, and a trade that empties the level meeting the queue ahead alone, its update landing at the trade's second or the one before; a mid instant in a stale stretch read at the first fresh second after it; the adverse-selection read at +60 s and +900 s against planted mids; an hour opening with an update and no carried book seeded from the panel's state of the hour before, or skipped and counted without it; a day's last hour filed under its own day, its mids read from the next; an hour the run's listing lacks and a second listing holds refusing the set, and a trades hour both lack admitted; a healed hour the run lists from its primary, its overlay directory moved aside, and the second listing from the overlay refusing the set; a root that cannot be read as a directory refusing the run and the manifest.

## D4 — the reading's computes half: the fill table and the reversal surface

- **The fill table**, per pair × side × size × horizon `H ∈ {60, 300, 900}` s, per month window (D2) and over the span: the fill rate (`filled` with `time_to_fill_s ≤ H` over posts), the median time to fill of the filled, and the adverse selection — the mean of the mid's move from `fill_price` to `mid_at_fill_plus_60s` and to `+900s`, signed against the order (a bid's fill followed by a lower mid is positive adverse selection), in bps, over the fills whose mid the data reaches, the others counted apart.
- **The reversal surface**: the mid at the posts gives a 15m series per pair (the post at each quarter-hour's boundary); its log return `r[t]` and trailing 96-bar scale σ (one day of 15m bars) as spec 00150 D3 defines them; at a bar whose `|r| > θ·σ`, `θ ∈ {1, 2}`, the trade is the passive order posted **against the move** at the bar's close — a bid after a down move, an ask after an up move — at size `S ∈ {€100, €1k}`, filled or not under the dataset's own rows (the post at that boundary, that side, that size), horizon 900 s. A **cell** is `(pair, θ, S, side)`: 12 × 2 × 2 × 2 = **96 cells**. Its reads: the fill rate among its signal bars; among the filled, the **gross return per filled trade** = the move from `fill_price` to `mid_at_post_plus_3600s` in the trade's favour, in return units — the fill at the touch price carries the half-spread captured into it, never added a second time; a filled trade whose `+3600 s` mid the data does not reach counts in the fill rate and is counted apart, out of `n` and the means; the **net** = gross − **0.0080**; per month window and over the span, `n` and the means; over the span alone, the bootstrap `p` of the mean net being at or below zero, `reality_check_pvalue([[g_k − 0.0080] for each filled trade k], mean_block=30, n_resamples=4000, seed=42)["p_value"]` (the floor 1/4001 under the Bonferroni bound 0.05/96 = 0.00052…).
- **Author's call — the hurdle is the maker round trip 0.0080, the spread credited**: a maker earns the half-spread and pays the fee; a taker's 0.016 is not the family's premise. **Author's call — the unfilled signal bars count as no trade**, not as a taker fill: the family is maker-only by its own definition, and a cell's fill rate is reported beside its return so a thin fill rate is read for what it is.

## D5 — the potential criterion, pre-registered

- **The floor**: a cell has at least 100 filled trades over the span and at least 20 in each of its three month windows (2026-08, 2026-09, 2026-10 through the 25th), counted as the `n` its means are over — a thinner cell is unread; a 2σ cell sees about 76–90 signal bars a month (reading 7's BTC/EUR counts), so a floor of 100 a month would leave every 2σ cell unread before any fill.
- **Potential** needs all four: (1) its fill rate at 900 s is at least 0.50 over the span — an order against the move fills only once trades on the move's side meet the queue ahead and the order, so the bars whose price turns first go unfilled, and below half the filled trades are a minority that selection picks, not the rule's typical signal; (2) its mean gross return per filled trade exceeds 0.0080 in each month window; (3) over the span; (4) its bootstrap p is below 0.05/96. A cell with potential names trial 1: that rule, the fill model as this dataset computes it, judged by the family's kill bar against the frozen 4h benchmark and a head-to-head against the adopted ensemble, a maker-fee cost model stated in that spec. None with potential is the evidence the owner's C1 family call reads; the reading closes nothing. The fill table (D4) is read beside the criterion, no criterion of this reading.
- **The contamination**: the reversal surface is a search over 96 cells on the whole span; a trial it names is designed on this data, counts C1's true trials with these 96 looks in its design, and its spec says so. The holdout is untouched: the capture starts 2026-07-08 and the holdout window ends 2026-07-09, so the capture's first 35 hours, 2026-07-08 13:00 → 07-09 23:00, lie inside the window; D2's span starts 2026-08-01, so no row of the dataset lies inside the window.

## Out of scope

Any trial; a taker cost model; partial fills, order identities or cancellations ahead credited (the level's quantity bounding the queue ahead, D2's fill rule, credits none); a posting price inside the spread — an order there is its side's best quote alone, and the captured prints were made against a book without it, so the prints that would have met it are not the captured ones; sizes above €1k — a full per-name position at the account's capital is about €1,000–1,400 (master plan §3); a horizon past one hour — §5's C1 is a 15m–1h reversal; the live execution scheduler — master plan §6's 15m execution layer, which this reading never changes; C3; any change to the capture pipeline, the panel materializer or an existing dataset.
