# A fleet-dark hour still missing a trades file waits for it, up to six hours, before the reconciler books it

Branch `feat/iter-214-reconcile-deferred-booking`, cut from `develop` at `3fb48bf8c` on 2026-11-27, iteration `iter-214`. The owner ruled the change on 2026-11-26 (D1–D3, D8); this spec designs it, and its plan is `docs/plans/00167-reconcile-deferred-booking.md`, written on the recommended options of D4–D7, which are OPEN for the owner's pick before `zcrypto-plan-review` reads the pair at tier `full`.

## Goal

A `both_streams_silent` hour in which a booked pair's trades file is not yet on either mirror is not booked when it is first examined but read again each `:12`/`:42` cycle until the file arrives or the hour is six hours old, and is then booked exactly as today.

## The ruling

Asked 2026-11-26T10:49:46Z, verbatim: "The cause is a race: an hour's trades file is closed only when the pair next trades, so a quiet pair's file can reach the NAS after the reconciler's two-hour settle has already booked the hour. What should the reconciler do when a fleet-dark hour still lacks a trades file for some pair?" The owner's answer by 10:51:44Z, verbatim: "Defer the booking, bounded (Recommended)", the option reading, verbatim: "A both-streams-silent hour with a missing trades file is not booked yet; it is re-read each cycle until the file arrives or a bound passes (I propose H+6 h), then booked as today. Its own short spec and plan, reviewed, landing through an attended converge of this workstation's ops role." The ask's other options are not in this branch's inputs.

- **Where the absence vetoes.** A `present` pair with no trades final on either mirror logs `archive reconcile: no trades segment on either mirror` and joins `unread`, and any `unread` entry refuses `venue_halt` (`cli/archive/command.py:925-931`); the record is decided once (`:862`, `_ledger`'s `seen`, `:735-748`).
- **When the file exists.** A pair's hour-H trades final is written when its first accepted print after H's end arrives (`cli/capture/segment_writer.py:286-308`, `:519-521`); a pair with no print in H writes none (`infra/runbooks/ops.md:273`). Under that cadence a final is on the NAS by about 1 h 10 min after it closes and read by the next tick, so the H+6:12 cycle reads a pair whose next print lands within about 4 h 01 min of H's end; the figure moves by one pull's duration, which is not measured, and the pull script records the loop once stretched to about 103 minutes (`pull-entrypoint.sh:234-236`), under which it is about 3 h 29 min.
- **Thin pairs.** Kraken's 15-minute bars for the twelve pairs, 2026-07-08 to 2026-10-27 (`data/ohlc-rolling`): the longest wait from a printed hour's end to the pair's next print is under 2 h 30 min (AVAX/EUR after 2026-07-15T22), none over 3 h; 25 of the 32,256 pair-hours had no print at all, so no trades final ever. - **A late tape can turn a refused proof into a held one.** The quiet proof's 0.0 sentinel (no print on one side of a window) rises past 240 s when the missing tape lands, and a twin tape can fill an id hole (the skeptic's `classify_halt` probe: `quiet` 0.0 → 915.0 and `holes` 1 → 0, each flipping `holds` to True). Only `carried` and the triage, which read book rows, and a print strictly inside a booked window are final on the tapes in hand.

On a fixture where BTC/EUR's primary resumes 30 s after its secondary inside a halt and ETH/EUR's trades file lands one cycle late, the skeptic measured a record-only deferral booking 610 s of loss and 610 s of halt, the residual page firing at the first look with no `both_streams_silent` record and no ERROR line (the mint logs at INFO, `:1082`); every tape on time books 0 s of loss and 1220 s of halt, today's code 1220 s of loss. - **A trades read that fails once is decided once.** `_tape` catches any exception into `unread` (`command.py:762-780`) over an NFS mount `soft,timeo=100,retrans=3` (`infra/ansible/roles/ops/tasks/main.yml:93`), so a transient read error books a provable halt as loss for good, while the same error on a book read fails the cycle and the next decides on a clean read (`command.py:844-848`). ## Operating model

- **The reconciler.** `zcrypto-archive-pull.service`, `Type=oneshot` (`archive-pull.service.j2:16`), started by its timer at `:12` and `:42`, runs one `zcrypto archive reconcile … --window-hours 48 --min-gap-seconds 30 --mint --textfile …` per tick in the ops image (`archive-pull.sh.j2:122-134`) after a gate on the NAS's `.pull-status` that skips the tick, rc 0, when the status is missing, unclean, over 4 h old or skewed (`:24-36`, `:77-95`).
- **The mirrors.** The NAS's copies of the two capture hosts, read over that NFS mount; a final appears in a cycle's scan once the NAS's pull of its host has carried it (`scan_hours` reads final names only, `settle.py:74-93`), the primary's copy ahead of the secondary's within a NAS loop.
- **The ledger.** `reconcile-ledger.jsonl` beside the overlay, written by this process alone, append-only, each `(pair, kind, hour, state)` decided once (`command.py:691`, `:735-748`); changed otherwise only by the owner-approved hand procedure (`infra/nas/README.md:148-185`).
- **What names a waiting hour, and when it is alive.** Nothing is stored for it: on every cycle an hour waits while it is in the 48-hour window, has fleet-dark windows, holds no `both_streams_silent` record, read every book segment, has a present pair whose trades final is on neither mirror (or, under D5's recommendation, one that failed to read), and `is_late` is false. A cycle that dies leaves nothing to clean, and a rolled-back image sees an unbooked hour, which it books on its next cycle as today.
- **The skip cache.** `scan-cache.json` beside the ledger keeps an hour only when its examination was late, complete, failure-free and appended nothing (`cli/archive/scan_cache.py:224-233`, `command.py:1198-1205`).

## Decisions

**D1. A fleet-dark hour with a missing trades file is not booked yet: no record, examined again each cycle.** The ruling: "A both-streams-silent hour with a missing trades file is not booked yet; it is re-read each cycle". Where the record is decided today, after the halt evidence is read, the pairs of `present` whose trades final is on neither mirror are listed; while that list is not empty and the hour is not late, nothing is ledgered and the cycle moves on; `_decided`, `settled_hours` and every later cycle's examination stay as they are, so "re-read each cycle" is the window the cycle already scans. "Missing" is the file the ruling's question names, one that has not reached the NAS: a final on either mirror has, and ends the wait, the halt proof already reading through one mirror (`tests/test_archive_reconcile_command.py:1599-1611`); waiting for its twin as well, which can fill an id hole, would hold every hour whose secondary copy trails the primary within a NAS loop.

**D2. The bound is the late deadline, H+6 h.** The ruling: "until the file arrives or a bound passes (I propose H+6 h)". The wait reads `late`, `is_late(hour, now)`, which the cycle already computes once per hour (`command.py:802`): the first `:12`/`:42` cycle at or past H+6 h books. One constant, `LATE_MINT_HOURS`, then holds both the heal's late deadline and this bound, the deadline `PANEL_SETTLE = 7 h` already takes as the reconciler's last mint (`cli/panel/materialize.py:41-46`), and the gate's 4 h status age was sized under it (`archive-pull.sh.j2:25-27`). The ruling names two ends and this spec adds no third: a clock-free end, such as the pair's later-hour trades final proving H printless, is not read. What the bound does not cover books at it as today: a pair with no print in H, a pair whose next print lands more than about four hours after H's end (none in the sample), a trades merge that failed on both hosts until a capture restart's sweep, and a NAS view the gate admits up to 4 h stale, which at H+6:12 can be the NAS as it stood at about H+2:12.

**D3. At the end of the wait the hour is booked as today, from that cycle's view.** The ruling: "then booked as today". The cycle that ends the wait runs today's code unchanged over what it reads: the present pairs, the fleet windows recomputed from every book stamp (a book mirror that landed meanwhile can shrink, split or erase a window, and an hour left with none books no record), the stream windows, the triage, the halt proof, the verdict, the line (WARNING for a halt, ERROR for loss) and the record with its pinned key set (`tests/test_archive_reconcile_command.py:1181`). A pair still without a trades final logs today's `no trades segment on either mirror` WARNING and refuses the halt, and the residual counter steps and both pages fire, on that cycle. No field says the booking waited.

**D4 (OPEN). The book heal of a waiting hour.** For the owner's pick; the plan is written on A.
- **A — it waits with the record (recommended).** While an hour waits, no pair of it enters the book heal: no `minted`, `would_mint`, `unwitnessed` or heal `failed` record for it. At the end of the wait the record is decided first and the heal subtracts the stream windows it booked, as on a first look today. Cost: a waiting hour's book mints and their failures land up to H+6:12 instead of H+2:12 — no later than today's late mints, inside `PANEL_SETTLE`; one line of code and one case. Its benefit is the skeptic's fixture, 0 s of loss and 1220 s of halt in place of 610 s and 610 s with a page that has no record to read; whether a real halt produces that fixture's skew is not established.
- **B — it runs as today.** Cost: wherever a pair's two mirrors resume apart inside a waiting halt, its unfilled seconds book as permanent loss on the first look, `zcrypto-reconcile-residual-gap` pages with no `both_streams_silent` record and no ERROR line, and each such booking is a hand correction whose 24 h `resets()` blind spot follows (`infra/nas/README.md:146`).

**D5 (OPEN). A trades final that fails to read.** The ruling names a missing file; the halt proof's `unread` also holds a present final that fails to read or holds a null, mistyped or unconvertible `ts` or `trade_id` (`command.py:762-780`). For the owner's pick; the plan is written on A.
- **A — it waits too, to the same bound (recommended).** The wait's condition is `missing or unread`, one term more. A read that fails on one cycle is read again on the next, as a book read already is. Cost: a final corrupt for good holds its hour's loss booking and page from H+2:12 to H+6:12, though the trades heal fails the cycle on that file where it reads one — the secondary's copy, or the primary's beside a secondary copy, until the pair-hour is minted (`command.py:1092-1108`) — and three hours of failed cycles page `zcrypto-reconcile-exporter-stale`; nine existing cases move their run from the first look to the bound.
- **B — only an absent file waits.** The ruling's words, nothing more. Cost: a transient read error on one trades file books a provable halt as loss once and for good, as above, and pages.

**D6 (OPEN). What shows a waiting hour.** For the owner's pick; the plan is written on A.
- **A — one WARNING per waiting cycle (recommended):** `archive reconcile: booking deferred hour=<H> until=<H+6 h> missing=<pairs or -> unreadable=<mirror pair, or -> -- a fleet-dark hour waits for these trades segments, then books whatever arrived`. At most eight lines an hour (H+2:12 to H+5:42), paging nothing (`zcrypto-ops-error-logs` reads ERROR and CRITICAL, `infra/grafana/alerts.yaml:2652`); no metric, no record. Cost: the wait is visible only in the unit's journal and Loki.
- **B — A and a gauge of the hours waiting.** Cost: the textfile's pinned series set grows (`tests/test_archive_reconcile_command.py:354`), the integrity board takes a panel (`tests/test_dashboards_cover_metrics.py`), and a Grafana push follows the converge; no rule reads it.
- **C — A and a ledger record per waiting hour.** Cost: a new state, moving no counter if it carries no `residual_seconds` (`command.py:405-410`), joins the README's state list (`README.md:231`) and the ledger-scan bench's (`infra/scripts/bench-ledger-scan.py:45`), and `ledger_records` counts it.

**D7 (OPEN). The skip cache's `ALGO_VERSION`.** Its comment asks a bump "whenever anything that decides an hour's verdict changes -- the examination logic" (`scan_cache.py:65-69`). For the owner's pick; the plan is written on A.
- **A — keep `ALGO_VERSION = 1` (recommended).** A wait happens only on an examination that is not late, which is never cached (`scan_cache.py:224-233`; `test_non_late_hour_is_never_cached`, `tests/test_archive_reconcile_command.py:1805-1822`), and a late examination decides exactly as today's code, so no entry either image caches is skipped wrongly by the other — spec 00163 D13's argument, which holds under every option of D4–D6.
- **B — bump it to 2.** The comment's letter. Cost: one cold cycle after the converge, every hour of the window re-examined and `hours_skipped` 0, its duration on the host not measured here against the `zcrypto-reconcile-cycle-duration` warning's 1500 s; one line, no case pinning the number (`tests/test_archive_scan_cache.py:415-420` reads the salt's `v` prefix).

**D8. It lands through an attended ops converge.** The ruling: "Its own short spec and plan, reviewed, landing through an attended converge of this workstation's ops role." *The converge* says how.

## The converge

The merge ships nothing: the reconciler runs the image `ops_image_digest` pins until an ops converge re-pins it, and the pages' new sentences describe an image not yet running until then. The converge is the owner's one `infra/ansible/scripts/converge.sh` line after the coordinator's `--check`, by `.claude/skills/zcrypto-rollout-image/SKILL.md`'s *Ops converges* and *Converge mechanics*: the ops image built from `develop` at or after the merge and pulled on `zcrypto-ops`; the running digest recorded in `docs/reference/fleet-pins.md` first, the rollback operand; `--limit zcrypto-ops --tags ops` with `-e ops_image_digest=…`, `-e liquidations_decision=roll-after` and the Alloy digest and two on-chain image ids the host runs, as the last ops image converge carried them (`docs/reference/deploy-log.jsonl`, 2026-11-23T11:45:32Z; `infra/runbooks/ops-node.md:489` says why the ids ride along); Kraken's `scheduled-maintenances.json` read whole at planning and again immediately before, `zcrypto-ops` being venue-facing (`.claude/rules/fleet-deploys.md`); never under `timeout`. No schema widens, no Alloy changes and no Grafana push is owed under D6's A. After the next tick, `bash infra/scripts/ops-postverify.sh`, every check, by value; then the deploy-log line committed with the re-trued pins row. The change's first real proof is the next fleet-dark hour with a thin leg: its deferral WARNINGs, then its booking. Rollback is the re-converge to the recorded digest; a waiting hour then books on the old image's next cycle at its first look, as today.

## What this change does not do

- **The book-mirror sibling.** A dark hour examined between the NAS's pull of the primary and of the secondary sees some pairs' book finals on one mirror only, which caps the triage at `undetermined` and fails `carried`, and is decided once as loss (`settle.py:340-345`, `:448-450`). Its frequency is not measured; this change, keyed on trades files, does not reach it. Whether it becomes a topic or is dropped is the owner's word, asked with D4–D7.
- **A zero-print pair.** It never gets a trades final, so its hour waits to the bound and still loses its halt there, as today.
- **Hand corrections already made.** The corrected hour 08 stays; the correction script's departures from `infra/nas/README.md`'s procedure (its copy-aside inside the replicated tree, no `scan-cache.json` deletion, no committed correction record; `:179`, `:183-184`) are the owner's to weigh and not this change's.
- **A clock-free end, a wait for a twin, a new metric, a new rule** (D1, D2, D6).
