# An order Kraken closes short of its size settles at the refused cancel, and the plan goes on

Branch `feat/t0229-closed-short-settles-inline`, cut from `develop` at `ac3064f6b` on 2026-11-16, for topic T0229. The owner ruled the fix's shape that day, "Inline, and the plan resumes" (D1); this spec designs it, and its plan is `docs/plans/00160-closed-short-settles-inline.md`, reviewed by `zcrypto-plan-review` at tier `full` (`infra/scripts/tier.py --paths` over the plan's files, its output in the plan's head) before its first task.

## Intent and the measured defect

Kraken can close an order with a remainder too small to trade: the order reads `closed`, reason `Insignificant volume remaining`, its `vol_exec` under its `vol`. On the pinned adapter (`nautilus-trader===2.0.0rc9.dev20261106`, `pyproject.toml:8`) no terminal event for it reaches the engine, so the engine's order stays open and partly filled until its intent's 15-minute box (`_TIME_BOX`, `cli/engine/executor.py:109`) sends a cancel; Kraken refuses that cancel `EOrder:Unknown order`, and the refused-cancel arm (`cli/engine/executor.py:4350-4368`) journals the intent `ambiguous` and drops every later intent of the plan (`_drop_remainder_after_ambiguity`, `:5098-5127`). The row stays `accepted` until a restart's startup pass reads Kraken's report and writes it `filled`, and nothing ever rewrites the intent.

The measured case, 2026-11-11 00Z, plan `r3-20261111-00` (*The measured basis*): DOT/EUR's sell `O-20261111-000249-001-000-4`, Kraken `ONMKDH-ERZI3-DMWCV4`, `vol` 5.18909578 and `vol_exec` 5.18909575, closed at 00:03:01.2Z; the box's cancel at 00:17:49.62Z was refused at 00:17:49.67Z, intent 3 ended `ambiguous` and intent 4, DOGE's sell, was refused `not run -- intent 3 ended ambiguous …` and sold at 04Z instead. The remainder, 3e-08, is three of DOT/EUR's lot steps (`lot_decimals` 8) and far under its `ordermin` of 3.9, so the engine's own completion test (`_on_fill`, `:4972-4980`, a remainder under one lot step) never fired.

This change reads Kraken's own record of the order at the refusal, settles the row and the intent `filled` when the order closed with the fill the engine recorded, and lets the plan run its next intent.

## Goals, non-goals

Goals:

- A cancel Kraken refuses on an order it has already closed with the engine's own fill ends the intent `filled` and the row `filled`, and the plan's next intent starts on the next tick, under that tick's own window, gate and box.
- Every other answer, a read that fails and a read the engine must not make leave the intent `ambiguous` and the plan dropped, exactly as today, and the CRITICAL line names what Kraken's record said, or why it was not read.
- A settled refusal pages nothing; the operator's page and the daily read say what each line means.
- The settled row no longer carries its symbol's leg in the next drafts or holds the watchdog's freeze.

Non-goals:

- The missing terminal event itself: an adapter defect, the `nautilus` peer's to raise upstream (D8).
- The Cache's stale open copy of the order, which nothing in this process can close (D7).
- Re-arming the re-read pass, the ruling's other option (D1), and the re-read pass's own population.
- Settling the row or the intent on any other answer (D1, D11).
- The cancel the startup pass or a trip sends for an adopted order (`_venue_terminal_state`, `:4520-4537`), whose refusal stays as it is.

## Operating model

- **The process.** One engine process, the `zcrypto-engine` container on `zcrypto`. The node runs every strategy callback on its main thread: the executor's 5-second tick (`_TICK_SECONDS`, `:102`; `cli/engine/node.py:299`, `:325-327`), its quotes, and its order events (`cli/engine/node.py:333-335`), one after another, never two at once (measured). No second thread or process writes anything this change reads or writes.
- **The executor's state.** In memory: the running plan (`_plan`), its index (`_index`) and the one active intent (`_active`, an `_ActiveIntent`, `:1043-1110`), which holds the live order (`client_order_id`, `order_qty`, `order_filled`) and, after this change, the live order's Kraken txid (`venue_order_id`). One intent is active at a time; its next starts only from the tick's `_pump` (`:2853-2867`) through `_start_intent` (`:2939-3016`).
- **The exec record.** `<journal>/<YYYY-MM-DD>/exec-<HH>.json`, one per boundary: its `plans` entries carry each intent's `outcome`, `reasons` and `filled_qty`, and its `submitted` rows each order's `state`, `filled_qty` and `events`. Written by read-modify-write with no lock, single-writer while every mutator runs on the node's one thread (`cli/engine/execledger.py`, `_load_or_new`'s docstring); the settle's writes run there too. A row is open while its state is `submitting`, `accepted` or `ambiguous` (`_OPEN_ORDER_STATES`, `cli/engine/execledger.py:30`), and the open rows of today's and yesterday's records (`open_submitted_rows`, `:297-302`) are what the draft's carry (`_draft_table`, `cli/engine/executor.py:3657`, `:3672-3679`), the watchdog's strand (`_stranded_rows`, `:1669-1680`) and the startup pass read.
- **The Cache.** The library's, backed by the cache set and restored at each start, holding each order and position by the engine's client order id and each order's txid by the venue-order-id index. The library applies an order event to the order and the Cache before the strategy sees it (`:1727-1732`). The executor reads it through a handle taken at construction (`:1141-1149`).
- **Kraken's record.** What `read_venue_orders(since)` answers: every order open, or closed since `since`, as the adapter's `OrderStatusReport`s, from a bare `KrakenSpotHttpClient` it builds per call on the trade credentials (`_bare_client`, `:655-668`), its reads run under `asyncio.run(asyncio.wait_for(…, 30 s))` and so synchronous to the caller. The startup pass reads it once per process (`_read_venue_orders`, `:2246-2286`), a failure refusing every plan until a restart; the re-read pass on a tick with the engine armed and nothing in flight (`:1460`, `_nothing_in_flight` `:1905-1916`), three tries (`_REREAD_ATTEMPTS`, `:233`). Both pass `self._venue_orders` when a test injects one.
- **What names an order across them.** The engine's client order id names the row, the Cache's order and the order events; Kraken's txid names Kraken's record, recorded in the row by its `OrderAccepted` event and, after this change, on the active intent; `(plan_id, intent_index)` names the intent. A report carries no client order id, only the txid (`tests/test_engine_executor.py`'s `_report` docstring).
- **The nonce.** The bare client signs on the key the execution client signs on, and the adapter serialises signed requests per client only (`:583-592`). At a refusal the execution client has nothing outstanding: the refusal is the answer to its last signed request, the cancel. What the in-flight read cannot see is the execution client's token request while its socket reconnects, the residual `read_venue_orders` already names.

## Decisions

**D1. At a cancel the venue refuses, the refusal's handler reads Kraken's record of the order; an order Kraken reports closed with the engine's fill ends its intent `filled` and the plan runs its next intent; every other answer, or a failed read, stays `ambiguous` with the remainder dropped as today.** The owner's ruling, on an ask the coordinator put on 2026-11-16, verbatim from `.tmp/t0229/inputs/asked.md`: asked at 2026-11-16T15:47:43.223Z, "T0229 (an order Kraken closes short of its size, e.g. DOT 5.18909575 of 5.18909578 on 11-11): today the 15-minute box's cancel meets `EOrder:Unknown order`, the intent ends ambiguous, the plan drops its later legs, and the row stays open until a restart. How should the fix settle it?", with two options — "Via the re-read pass (Recommended)": "At the refusal nothing changes (intent ambiguous, later legs dropped). It also arms the existing re-read pass, which on the next quiet tick reads Kraken's report and settles the row and the intent filled at vol_exec. That ends the open-until-restart row, the same-day carried leg and the watchdog strand. The dropped legs draft again at the next boundary. No new path on the order-event handler." — and "Inline, and the plan resumes": "The refusal's handler reads Kraken's record itself. On closed-with-a-fill, the intent ends filled and the plan runs its next leg, so the boundary loses nothing. It adds a synchronous venue read on the event path and a resume after a refused cancel, which the live trade path has never had." Answered at 2026-11-16T15:48:55.449Z: "Inline, and the plan resumes". The re-read pass is not armed for it, and this spec does not re-open that option; it reuses the startup pass's and the re-read pass's reader, `read_venue_orders`, and their terms.

**D2. The read runs in the refused-cancel arm itself, on the node's main thread, synchronous, bounded by the reader's own `_VENUE_READ_TIMEOUT_SECONDS`, 30 s.** The owner's "synchronous venue read on the event path" (D1); the bound is the author's. Measured (*The measured basis*): the order event runs on the main thread with no asyncio loop running, so the reader works there as in the tick; for the read's duration nothing else of the process runs, and the order events, quotes and timer firings that land meanwhile are delivered after it, in order — the missed timer firings in one burst, which the tick tolerates, each firing reading the same clock. What the stall can hold up: nothing of this process's own can be live beside the refused intent, one intent being active at a time and its plan's next intent waiting on this one either way; the boundary's alert cannot fall inside it, every order ending by about B+3h47 (`_SUBMISSION_WINDOW`'s comment, `:119-122`); a socket's `DISCONNECTED` landing inside it is stamped when it is delivered (`on_socket_state`, `:1555`), so the watchdog's 30 s grace (`_SOCKET_DOWN_GRACE`, `:129`) runs from up to 30 s after the drop and its freeze comes that much later. If a future wheel ran an asyncio loop on that thread, `asyncio.run` would raise, the read would fail and the intent stay `ambiguous`: the closed side. Set aside: the next tick, the same thread and the same bound, plus a phase to hold the intent in until then and up to 5 s more; a worker thread collected by a tick, which frees the main thread at the cost of a thread the engine has nowhere else, a bare client off the main thread no reading covers, and the same new phase; a tighter bound of its own, a guess against an unmeasured latency whose only effect would be more `ambiguous` intents.

**D3. The read is made for every `OrderCancelRejected`, whatever its reason, and only on `read_venue_orders`' terms: the engine armed, nothing of this process in flight in the Cache's terms, and the live order's txid known. `OrderCancelDenied` reads nothing.** The author's call. Kraken's answer decides, never its error text: a refusal of an order still open reads ACCEPTED and stays `ambiguous` as today, so reading on `EService:Busy` costs one bounded read and changes nothing; keying on `EOrder:Unknown order` would make a string written for people decide a venue read. A denied cancel never reached Kraken, its order resting unless it filled, and its CRITICAL line stays as it is. The terms:

- **Armed** (`_gate_armed`, the newest evaluation's): the attended passes that sign on the engine's key run with it disarmed, the re-read pass's rule (`:1451-1461`). A disarm's own revoke, whose cancel Kraken refuses, therefore stays `ambiguous`; a kill file leaves the engine armed, so its revoke's refusal reads.
- **Nothing in flight**, `orders_inflight(venue=KRAKEN)` empty: the refused order no longer counts (measured: PARTIALLY_FILLED, in-flight 0), so a non-empty set is another order of this process with a cancel unanswered — a trip's, or the startup pass's of an adopted order — whose request shares the key. A Cache that cannot answer holds the read, as `_nothing_in_flight` holds the re-read pass. The `_active is None` half of `_nothing_in_flight` does not apply: the refused intent is its whole subject.
- **The txid**: `_ActiveIntent.venue_order_id`, set from the live order's `OrderAccepted` (`:4311-4329`) and cleared by `_place` for each new order (`:3145-3151`), so a replacement whose acceptance has not landed reads no earlier order's record. An order with no acceptance names nothing to read.
- **Since**: the running plan's boundary less `_VENUE_READ_MARGIN`, one hour (`:228`), the startup pass's and the re-read pass's rule for a row filed under that boundary.

**D4. One answer settles: a report for the txid whose status is FILLED — Kraken's `closed`, whatever its reason — with a fill above `_OVERFILL_TOLERANCE`, 1e-12 (`:134`), and within 1e-12 of the fill this process recorded for the live order (`active.order_filled`). Every other answer leaves the intent `ambiguous` and the row as it is.** The owner's "closed-with-a-fill" (D1), made exact by the author. Whatever its reason: the adapter reads every `closed` as FILLED (measured), and the startup pass already writes `filled` from any FILLED report (`_ADOPTED_TERMINAL_STATES`, `:187-194`), so the inline settle writes what a restart would. A fill: `closed` with nothing executed reads FILLED too (measured), and an order that filled nothing is no closed-with-a-fill. The recorded fill: a figure above it is a fill the stream has not delivered, and a figure below it a fill Kraken no longer reports; the startup pass owns both — its repair (`reconciled`) and its withdrawal trip, with the trade-history read behind it (`_reconcile_adopted_row`, `:2429-2549`) — and a repair here would put the row ahead of the Cache's position, which the terminal's reconciliation (`_reconcile_terminal`, `:4191-4237`) holds the intent's fills to and trips the kill switch on. The tolerance is the startup pass's own dead-band for a sum of per-fill floats against one exactly rounded figure. Set aside: the reason `Insignificant volume remaining` alone, a string deciding a state; a repair to Kraken's figure, the kill trip above. The answers and what each does are *The answers*.

**D5. A settled refusal writes the row `filled` with a `venue_closed` event, counts the order `filled`, logs one WARNING and ends the intent through `_finish_active("filled", (), active.filled)`; the plan's next intent starts on the next tick through `_start_intent`, and `_drop_remainder_after_ambiguity` is not reached.** The author's call. The row: `state` `filled`, `filled_qty` untouched (the figure agrees, D4), and one event `{"event": "venue_closed", "at", "venue_order_id", "venue_filled_qty", "venue_qty", "reason"}`, the record of what Kraken said, after the `OrderCancelRejected` event the arm writes first; `tracking.extract_fills` counts `fill` and `reconciled` events alone (`cli/engine/tracking.py:153-181`), so the event moves no figure. The counter: `zcrypto_exec_orders_total{outcome="filled"}` once, where today the arm counts `ambiguous`, and `_on_fill` never counted the order's completion, its remainder being three lot steps. `_finish_active` (`:5061-5079`) unsubscribes the quotes, journals the intent `filled` at the intent's recorded fills through `_journal_intent`, which re-sets the leg's gap gauge (`_set_cycle_gap`), clears `_active` and advances `_index`, then runs `_reconcile_terminal`: no longer an ambiguous exit, the venue having answered, the settled intent is held to the Cache's strategy-scoped position like any terminal, and a disagreement trips the kill switch. The resume is the tick's ordinary start of an intent (`_pump`, `:2853-2867`; `_start_intent`, `:2939-3016`): a submission window closed meanwhile carries the intent `carried` (`:2945-2955`), a kill file or a disarm refuses it at the gate (`:2965-2968`), the per-intent belt and the venue-truth read run, and the intent gets a 15-minute box of its own (`:3011`). It applies in every mode, a drill's `rest-cancel` or `rest-hold` intent included: an order Kraken closed with a fill filled, and one arm serves them all. It applies whatever asked for the cancel, the box or a revoke: a revoke's order Kraken closed with the recorded fill ends as one whose last fill lands while the revoke's cancel is out (`_on_fill`, `:4979-4980`), never through `_finish_revoked`'s halt (`:5081-5085`). The next start re-reads a revoke's gate level and the watchdog's freeze (`_evaluate` through `_fold_holds`, `:1331-1365`); quote silence, the one revoke reason it does not re-read, was the settled intent's own instrument's, and the next intent waits on its own instrument's quote (`_QUOTE_WAIT`, `:103`) and is revoked on that one's silence (`:2913-2915`).

**D6. A settled refusal logs one WARNING and pages nothing; the refused-cancel CRITICAL line names what Kraken's record said, or why it was not read; the denied line is unchanged; the error-logs runbook gains the line's class and the daily read the settled row's reading.** The author's call. `zcrypto-engine-error-logs` pages on ERROR and CRITICAL lines of `container="engine"` (`infra/grafana/alerts.yaml:592-651`), WARNING never. Today the measured case logged two CRITICAL lines, `cancel of … was REJECTED by the venue -- the order may still rest; the plan stops here` and `plan … dropped after intent 3 ended ambiguous -- 1 later intent(s) will not run`. After this change:

## Failure modes

- **The read stalls the main thread up to 30 s.** Nothing of the process runs meanwhile; events and timer firings are delivered after it in order (measured), the missed firings in one burst. Bounded by the reader's own timeout, after which the intent is `ambiguous`, today's outcome.
- **The read collides with the execution client's own signed request** — its WebSocket token request while its socket reconnects. One of the two is answered `Invalid nonce`: the library loses one reconnect attempt, or the read fails and the intent stays `ambiguous` (row 7). The library's retry of a refused token request is unmeasured, the residual `read_venue_orders`' docstring already names (`:589-592`).
- **Kraken closed the order and the stream has not delivered its last fill.** Kraken's figure is above the recorded one, row 4: `ambiguous`, and the late fill lands on the row through `_on_detached_event` when it arrives; a restart's startup pass settles the row.
- **The Cache's position disagrees with the settled intent's fills.** `_reconcile_terminal` trips the kill switch, the plan halts with the kill's reason and `_cancel_resting` cancels what the Cache holds open, the stale copy included, which Kraken refuses; the right side to fail on.
- **The ledger refuses the settle's write.** Row 12: the intent `ambiguous` through the arm's own writes. A ledger that refuses every write refuses the arm's first, the refusal's event, before the read, so the arm logs no line, where today its CRITICAL comes first; the order-event catch-all's ERROR pages in its place, and the cancel's 30 s ack deadline strands the intent, as it does today.
- **A future wheel delivers the terminal event.** The order closes before its box, the box sends no cancel, and the arm never runs; nothing here needs undoing.
- **A future wheel runs an asyncio loop on the event thread.** `asyncio.run` raises inside the reader, row 7: `ambiguous`, today's outcome, the CRITICAL line naming `RuntimeError`.
- **Kraken answers `closed` for an order it closed with a remainder above its minimums.** Not a shape Kraken is known to produce, `closed` meaning executed; were it read, the intent ends `filled` at Kraken's figure and the plan goes on, the venue state the next intents were authorized against being known, which is the ambiguity's only reason to stop a plan.
