# A restored order Kraken still lists open while the Cache reads it closed is cancelled at Kraken by its txid

## Intent and the measured defect

After an engine restart with an engine order resting at Kraken, the order comes back from the cache set as a restored copy under the engine's own client order id (spec 00120 D6). A part-fill's `trade` frame that lands on the private stream before the startup pass's cancel, and carries no `order_qty`, rewrites that copy to its filled quantity and `FILLED`, while the same frame on an order this process placed, its acknowledgement received or withheld, is booked once and leaves its copy open (both measured, *The measured basis*): the adapter takes the frame's quantity from `order_qty`, then from its own per-process cache keyed by `cl_ord_id`, which is empty after a restart, then from the filled quantity — the `nautilus` peer's reading of upstream's source, not run (`.local/reads-archive/feat/engine-arm-2.0.0rc9.dev20261106/step3/nautilus-retrace.md:47`). The startup pass's classification reads the Cache's open orders alone (`cli/engine/executor.py:1754`), so it never sees the closed copy and sends no cancel; the strategy handle's cancel would send nothing anyway for a closed order; no later start cancels it; and a later fill on it is rejected inside the library with no event. The remainder rests at Kraken with no time box, and only Kraken's open-orders page shows it.

Measured on the tree this branch starts from (*The measured basis*): a restored buy of 1.0 SOL/EUR, a frame without `order_qty` of 0.4 landing 0.53 s after the strategy started, reads `FILLED qty=0.4 filled=0.4` in the Cache, 0 `CancelOrder` calls, the order open at Kraken at `vol_exec` 0.4 of 1, the row `accepted` and the intent `pending`, and reads the same at the next start.

This change compares, in the startup pass, Kraken's report of each restored row's order with the Cache's copy of it, and cancels at Kraken, by its txid, an order Kraken lists open while the copy reads closed.

## Goals, non-goals

Goals:

- A restored order of this engine's ledger that Kraken reports open while the Cache's copy reads closed is cancelled at Kraken at the startup pass, whatever the frame's shape that closed the copy and whatever the row's `reduce_only`.
- Its row ends `canceled` with a `recancelled` event, and its figure reaches Kraken's final one: a fill that races the cancel, which the library drops against the closed copy, is booked by the re-read pass's read after it, which also writes the intent.
- The frame landing before the startup pass's cancel and inside it, with and without `order_qty`, is proven on the loopback against the pinned wheel, the owner's word (D2).
- The cancel logs one WARNING, which pages nothing; a cancel that fails logs one CRITICAL naming the hand cancel; the operator's restart read says what each line means.

Non-goals:

- The real `trade` frame's capture and nautilus_trader #5315's triggered fill: one attended session later, on a key the owner mints, in a gap the owner picks (D2).
- The double booking itself, a frame with `order_qty`, or one inside a cancel (D11).
- A restored copy closed by a terminal this engine minted, which the startup pass reports CRITICAL for a hand cancel today: left as it is, on the owner's ruling (D15).
- An order the Cache does not hold at all, a row the ledger already reads closed, and an engine running without the cache (D5).
- A copy a frame closes after the startup pass, in the same process (D6).

## Operating model

- **The process.** One engine process, the `zcrypto-engine` container on `zcrypto`. The node restores the Cache from the cache set and runs its reconciliation before the trader starts; the strategy's `on_start` builds the executor, which reads the restored set (`_read_restored`, `cli/engine/executor.py:1279-1320`); every strategy callback runs on the node's main thread, the 5-second tick among them (spec 00160's measurement).
- **The startup pass.** `_adopt_resting_orders` (`:1687-1849`), on the first tick: the ledger's open and finished rows of the two-day window; one read of Kraken's orders for the rows that need it (`_read_venue_orders`, `:2251-2291`), a failure refusing every plan for the process's life; the holdings settle; the open-row sweep (`_reconcile_adopted_rows`, `:2044-2167`) and the finished-row sweep (`:2556-2629`); then the classification over `orders_open` (`:1754`, `:1807-1848`), whose cancels go through the strategy handle (`:1840`); then the window's `pending` intents (`_settle_pending_intents`, `:1851-1908`).
- **The re-read pass.** `_reread_pass` (`:1923-2025`), on a tick whose gate reads armed with nothing of this process in flight (`:1465`), armed by a mint or a restored row's fill (`_arm_reread_after_mint`, `:1594-1600`; `_fill_credit`, `:4961-4975`): it reads Kraken for the open rows a mint closed and the rows of `_restored_fills`, open or closed (`:1958-1972`), repairs them from Kraken's report, and writes a restored row's intent once a report has answered it (`_settle_restored_intent`, `:2380-2417`).
- **The ledger row.** `<journal>/<YYYY-MM-DD>/exec-<HH>.json`'s `submitted` entry: `state`, `filled_qty`, `events`; open while `submitting`, `accepted` or `ambiguous`. Its `OrderAccepted` event carries the order's txid.
- **The Cache.** The library's, backed by the cache set and restored at each start: the order under the engine's client order id, its txid in the venue-order-id index. A restored copy is the previous process's view, or the library's rewrite of it; `_cached_order` (`:2320-2329`) withholds a copy a minted terminal closed. The library rejects a fill that would take a closed copy past its quantity, with no event.
- **Kraken's record.** `read_venue_orders(since)`'s `OrderStatusReport`s, keyed by txid: Kraken's `open` reads `ACCEPTED` whatever `vol_exec` says, `closed` reads `FILLED`, `canceled` `CANCELED`, `expired` `EXPIRED` (spec 00160's measurement). `cancel_venue_order` (`:633-654`) sends `CancelOrder` by txid on its own bare client and returns on Kraken's answer without reading its `count`.
- **What names an order across them.** The engine's client order id names the row, the Cache's copy and the order events; the txid names Kraken's record, read for a row by `_read_id` (`:2358-2361`), the row's own txid else its restored copy's; a report carries no client order id. A row is restored when the Cache held its order at construction (`_restored_row`, `:2367-2371`), and the venue is asked over every restored row (`_venue_answers`, `:2347-2356`).

## Failure modes

- **Kraken refuses the cancel, or the cut is not over for REST.** The CRITICAL names the hand cancel; the row stays open at the report's figure; the re-read pass is armed all the same (D7) and reads the order once, sending no cancel (a read that fails on three ticks pages the re-read pass's `could not read the ledger or the venue on 3 ticks` CRITICAL, naming the same order and the same hand cancel): one Kraken reports ended — a remainder that filled whole before the cancel arrived — has its row and intent written from Kraken's record, and one Kraken still lists open keeps its row open; past the process, the next start inside the row's two UTC days writes the row from Kraken's record, or sends the cancel again if the order still rests.
- **Kraken answers the cancel without ending the order** — `{"count": 0}` for an open order, a shape not known of Kraken. The row reads `canceled`; the re-read pass reads the order open, repairs the figure and writes the intent, and sends no second cancel (D6); the rung-3 restart item 4's open-orders read lists the order, and the operator cancels it by hand.
- **A fill races the cancel.** The library drops it against the closed copy (measured); the re-read pass books it on the row and writes the intent at the final figure; the Cache's copy and its position stay short of it, the library's, and the position gauge takes Kraken's figure at that pass's settle.
- **The row write after the cancel fails.** The CRITICAL `the re-cancel of … could not be journaled`; the order is cancelled at Kraken, its row still open; the re-read pass is armed all the same (D7) and writes the row and its intent from Kraken's closed record, and where that write fails too, the next start inside the row's two UTC days reads the row against Kraken's closed record and writes it.
- **The startup read fails.** No report reaches the arm; every plan is refused; the remainder rests until a restart inside the row's two UTC days reads, and past them until a hand cancel (D9).
- **The cancel collides with the execution client's own signed request** — its token request while its socket reconnects, or the cancels a kill trip earlier in the same sweep has just sent (D4). One of the two is answered `Invalid nonce`: the cancel raises (the first mode), or the library loses one reconnect attempt, the residual `read_venue_orders`' docstring names, or the trip's cancel of an order is the one refused and that order may keep resting behind the trip's page; Kraken's answer to either collision is unmeasured.
- **The engine stays disarmed after the restart.** The re-read pass waits for a tick that reads it armed (`:1456-1461`); the row reads the report's figure and the intent stays `pending` until then; the cancel itself has gone.
- **A frame lands before the executor exists.** No fill reaches the row on the event path; the arm still reads the closed copy and Kraken's open report, cancels, and arms the read itself.
