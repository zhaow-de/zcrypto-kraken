## The question (no hypothesis, no bar)

Master plan §5's C2: BTC's intraday move leads the alts', refuted at the daily horizon, cheap to test on 15m bars, low prior, fee-fragile. This reading asks the fee question first: **after a 15m or 1h move in BTC/EUR beyond one or two of its own trailing daily scales, does any of the eleven followers' gross return over the next 15m or 1h, taken in the move's direction, exceed 0.012, the round trip at the registered 0.006 a side (D4) — in every year the potential criterion's floor counts (D5) and over the whole span?** A cell that clears the potential criterion (D5) names the trial a later spec would pre-register; none clearing it is the evidence the owner's C2 family call reads. The reading tests no hypothesis, has no bar, matrix or verdict, and spends no C slot.

## D3 — the leader's signals

- **The return**: `r[t] = ln(close_B[t] / close_B[t−1])` where the step from stamp `t−1` to `t` is exactly 15 minutes, else `None`.
- **The scale**: `σ[t]`, the sample standard deviation of `r` over the trailing 96 bars ending at `t` (one day of 15m bars), `None` until 96 returns are present (polars `rolling_std(window_size=96, min_samples=96)` over `r`, a `None` inside the window making it `None`), warm at leader index 2,831 (the basis).
- **The move**: `x_L[t] = Σ r[t−L+1 .. t]` for `L ∈ {1, 4}` — the last 15 minutes, the last hour — `None` if any term is `None`.
- **The signal**: at threshold `θ ∈ {1, 2}`, `up` if `x_L[t] > θ · σ[t] · √L`, `down` if `x_L[t] < −θ · σ[t] · √L`, else none; `None` scale or move gives none. The √L scales the one-bar standard deviation to an L-bar sum under independence — an author's call, the one choice that makes the 1σ threshold mean one scale at both lags.
- **Measured**: the counts in the basis, per (L, θ, side) and year, on the leader's grid before any follower is read.

## D4 — the followers' forward returns and the cells

- **The forward return**: for follower `f` and horizon `H ∈ {1, 4}` bars, `y_{f,H}[t] = ln(close_f(t + H·15min) / close_f(t))`, defined when `f` has a close at both stamps and at every leader stamp between, each step 15 minutes; else `None`. **Author's call — entry at the signal bar's close**: the signal is computed from the bar closing at `t` and the position is taken at that close — a fill at the instant the leader's bar prints, which no execution has (the next bar's open is the nearest real entry); a trial this reading names enters one bar after the signal (D5) and reads a different return, which this reading does not bound: an effect spent inside the bar after the signal reads here and is lost to that trial, and one that builds after it reads there and not here.
- **A cell** is `(f, L, θ, H, side)`: eleven followers × `L ∈ {1, 4}` × `θ ∈ {1, 2}` × `H ∈ {1, 4}` × `side ∈ {up, down}` = **176 cells**. Its **trades** are the leader's signal bars `t` of `(L, θ, side)` at which `y_{f,H}[t]` is defined; each trade's **gross return** is `y` for `up` and `−y` for `down` — long the follower after an up move, short it after a down move — and its **net return** is the gross less **0.012**, the round trip at 0.006 a side — the registered basis every B trial was judged at, the tier-1 maker fee 0.0040 plus 0.0020 of spread headroom (`CrossfreqSystemConfig`, `docs/reference/kraken-fee-schedule.md`), not a taker cost: a tier-1 taker round trip is 0.016 plus the spread, so a cell clearing 0.012 is not yet a cell a taker clears. Trades overlap when signals fall within `H` bars of each other; each is counted as its own trade. **Author's call — overlapping trades counted each as one**: merging them into one held position is a rule's design, with its own turnover, and belongs to the trial the reading names, not to the reading; counted apart, each signal pays the full round trip, which is the fee question asked at its hardest.
- **The reads per cell**: per calendar year of the leader's grid — `n` trades, the mean gross return per trade, the mean net return per trade, the means absent at `n` 0; over the whole span — `n`, the mean gross, the mean net, and the **bootstrap p**: `reality_check_pvalue([[g_k − 0.012] for each trade k in stamp order], mean_block=30, n_resamples=4000, seed=42)["p_value"]` — one row per trade, one strategy column, the utility's periods × strategies shape — the stationary bootstrap's p for the mean net return per trade being at or below zero, the repository's one reality-check utility over a one-model matrix; absent at fewer than 2 trades, which the utility refuses. No kill bar, no matrix and no verdict.
- **The context rows, no criterion reads them**: per follower, per `(L, H)` and per calendar year — the Spearman rank correlation between `x_L[t]` and `y_{f,H}[t]` over every leader stamp at which both are defined, the raw lead-lag effect with no threshold; and the follower's unconditional mean `y_{f,H}` per year, the drift a conditional mean is read against.
- **Refused before any forward return is computed**: a close that is not a positive number in any of the twelve files; a follower with no stamp on the leader's grid; a leader grid with a duplicate stamp; a cell is never refused for having no trade — it records `n` 0 and no mean, and the criterion's floor leaves it unread.

## D5 — the pre-registered potential criterion, and what the reading changes

- **The criterion**, spec 00141 D8's form — a floor and then legs — fixed by this spec's sha256 before any forward return conditioned on a signal is read, read by the closeout's pick off a scratch program's table printed from the run's line — every cell the floor admits, its three legs' values and whether each holds (D7) — and never by the subject's code: the subject has no rule. **The floor**: the cell has at least 100 trades in each **complete** calendar year its follower spans within **2015 through 2025** — from the first calendar year after the year of the follower's first stamp, or 2015 if later, through 2025; a follower whose first stamp is in 2025 or later spans no such year and every cell of it is unread. **Author's call — the floor at 100 per complete year, the years 2015–2025**: a per-year mean over fewer trades is a handful of bars; 2013 and 2014 are the leader's thin years (the basis: 2014 has 46–98 signals at 2σ and 291–369 at 1σ, 2013 fewer than 10), left out as the family kill bar leaves its stub years out, so that LTC's 2σ cells — LTC the one follower spanning 2014 — are read on the same years as every other follower's; from 2015 on, 100 admits every cell of the leader's own counts (the basis: the smallest count from 2015 at 2σ is 2015's 267 at L=4 up), so the floor decides only on a follower's coverage, which it is meant to. The complete years are the criterion's — per follower, from the basis's first stamps, every follower's last stamp falling in 2026: ADA/EUR 2019–2025, AVAX/EUR 2022–2025, DOGE/EUR 2020–2025, DOT/EUR 2021–2025, ETH/EUR 2016–2025, LINK/EUR 2020–2025, LTC/EUR 2015–2025, SOL/EUR 2022–2025, XRP/EUR 2018–2025, ETH/BTC 2017–2025, SOL/BTC 2022–2025 — and every year of the leader's grid is still recorded (D6). A cell under the floor is **unread**: no leg is read of it, and it has no potential. A cell the floor admits has **potential** only if all three legs hold, each read unrounded off its stored keys:
  1. its mean gross return per trade exceeds 0.012 in every year the floor counts;
  2. its mean gross return per trade over the whole span exceeds 0.012;
  3. its bootstrap p is below 0.05 / 176 = 0.000284090909… — Bonferroni over the 176 cells at 5 %. At 4,000 resamples the smallest p the utility returns is 1 / 4,001 = 0.000249937…, so the leg holds only at a bootstrap count of 0 — no resample's mean net return per trade at or above twice the cell's.
- **Author's call — Bonferroni over the 176 cells, though they are not independent** — a follower's two horizons share every trade's first bar, the two thresholds nest (every 2σ signal is a 1σ signal), and the EUR followers share BTC's own move in their EUR price: the count is the design's, fixed before the run, and over correlated cells it is conservative. **Author's call — legs 1 and 2 on the gross, not the net**: the leg asks whether the effect clears the cost, and "gross exceeds 0.012" is that question with no second constant.
- **A cell with potential names the trial a later spec would pre-register**: the rule on that cell — the follower long after an up move or short after a down move at that lag, threshold and horizon, entered one bar after the signal, overlapping signals merged into one held position — on the follower alone, judged by the family's kill bar (`a1_kill_bar` at the family's count, the 4h benchmark's calendar-year slices) and a head-to-head against the adopted A2-4h ensemble, net of 0.006 a side on its own turnover; the later spec states how and cites this reading. **None with potential** is the evidence the owner's C2 family call — hold or close C2 — reads; the reading closes nothing (D1).
- **The contamination, in full**: the reading is a search over 176 cells on the whole history of the set, the data any trial it names would be judged on; a trial it names is designed from this data, its spec says so, carries this reading's id, and counts the family's true trials in its DSR with this reading's 176 looks included in its design. The entry at the signal bar's close is a fill no execution has (D4). The holdout is untouched: the set ends 2026-03-31, the day before the window opens, and no cell reads `ohlc-holdout-*` or `ohlc-rolling`.
- **What it changes**: no verdict, no C slot, no record; its figures go to T0016's findings beside its decisions-log entry, and C2's next step after it — the pick reading this criterion, the loop's under a run instruction and else the owner's, a close the owner's — is T0016's C2 arm, rewritten to wait on a `[iter-NNN] C2's next step after the lead-lag reading` entry, written only when that pick is made, never as a default the reading's own entry carries.
