# The deployable's basket has diverged from the refreshed universe — re-ratify or hold

## Context — what

The engine trades record 44's ratified basket: ten EUR legs, a code constant derived from `cli/ohlc/fetch.py::PAIR_KEYS` in `cli/engine/store.py`, connected to no data artifact. The 2026-08-13 universe refresh (iter-137, `universe-20260813`) selected a different eleven: **DOT/EUR out** (median quote volume 146,957.37 vs the 150,000 floor — a real ~25% decline in three weeks, not a measurement artifact) and **ETH/BTC + SOL/BTC in** (both clear the floor comfortably once FX-normalised). Spec `00089` rules that the traded basket stays record 44's ten legs and makes the divergence *observable* — a repo-side concordance test with DOT `traded-but-deselected` and the two /BTC legs `selected-but-unreachable` as its ruled baseline, each exception citing this topic — but whether the basket ever *changes* is a ratification decision no read-only spec can close. The owner kept it genuinely open ("maybe" the /BTC legs go live during 6b) rather than freezing the basket for the phase.

## Why this matters

Two exposures, opposite in direction. While DOT stays in the basket, the executor can size a name the universe has judged too thin — dormant today (the strategy targets DOT at exactly 0.0, and 6b's rung sizes sit well under the €1,400 footprint the floor was calibrated against), but it does not stay dormant by construction. And while the /BTC legs stay unreachable, the ratified strategy surface is narrower than the selected universe: the two relative-value legs the universe considers tradeable cannot be expressed, because the engine is built EUR-only. **The reason first recorded here was wrong, and the survey below corrected it**: the obstacle is not the adapter's `margin_balance_asset` (one call site, account-summary denomination only, and the OpenPositions branch is quote-agnostic — the existing client already sees both legs) but the engine's own base-keyed symbol assumptions, `root/<base>/EUR/` store paths, EUR-NAV sizing without an FX term, and a EUR-denominated cost floor. A "yes" on the /BTC half is therefore still not a config edit — but the work is an engine re-plumb, not adapter surgery, and no second exec client is possible anyway (the venue registration refuses one).

Un-observed, either divergence could silently widen: that part is closed by `00089`'s concordance surface, which turns any *new* selection shift into a red test. What remains here is purely the decision.

## Findings so far

- The basket-to-universe isolation is deliberate and proved itself 2026-08-13: the refresh changed the artifact and the engine's basket did not move. `00089` writes that property down instead of leaving it implicit.
- DOT's exclusion is marginal (2% below the floor) and the floor is a footprint-sizing rule (a full €1,400 max-size position ≈ 1% of median daily volume). At 6b rung sizes the economic exposure of keeping DOT is nil; the exposure is structural, not financial, until the ramp.
- The /BTC legs' unreachability is architectural: `spot_positions_quote_currency`/`margin_balance_asset` are single-valued, and the EUR book is the ratified one. Any solution (second exec client, account-per-quote, adapter change) is real design work with its own blast radius. *(Superseded in part by the 2026-08-14 survey below: the adapter is not the obstacle — the engine's own EUR plumbing is.)*
- Record 44 is the ratified deployable; changing the basket re-opens its ratification, not just a constant.

### Multi-quote solve survey (2026-08-14, offline — the autonomous sub-item)

Evidence base: the installed nautilus-trader 1.230.0 (`.venv` sources incl. `execution/engine.pyx`, `live/node_builder.py`, `adapters/kraken/*`), the adapter's Rust sources cross-checked against the compiled `nautilus_pyo3` binary by distinctive string literals, two loopback probes through the real compiled parse paths (127.0.0.1-only canned Kraken responses, zero external traffic; scripts `t0137_loopback_probe.py` / `t0137_two_clients_probe.py`, session scratchpad — decisive outputs reproduced here), and the committed refdata snapshot `data/snapshots/kraken-refdata-20260804T104009Z.json`.

- **Two exec clients cannot coexist against one venue — and the constraint was constructed and seen to trip** *(confidence: high)*. `KrakenExecutionClient.__init__` hardcodes `venue=KRAKEN_VENUE`; `ExecutionEngine.register_client` (venue-routing branch of `execution/engine.pyx`) refuses a second client whose venue is already routed. Probe: two clients with distinct ids (`KRAKEN`, `KRAKEN2`) → first registers, second raises `ValueError: Execution client for venue Venue('KRAKEN') already registered (ClientId('KRAKEN'))`. The `TradingNodeConfig.exec_clients` dict key is no way around it: `node_builder.py` splits the key at the first hyphen and the prefix becomes both the factory-lookup name and the client name, so keys `KRAKEN` + `KRAKEN-XBT` build two identically-named clients that collide even earlier (duplicate `ClientId`). Command routing *would* support two clients per venue (priority: explicit `client_id` → account-issuer → venue map → default), but registration blocks coexistence before routing matters.
- **The two-client design is also unnecessary: `margin_balance_asset` is display-only and, under MARGIN, nothing in the adapter filters by quote currency** *(confidence: high)*. `node.py`'s "currently unread" comment is about `spot_positions_quote_currency` and is **confirmed**: the Rust `request_position_status_reports` consults `quote_currency` only in the non-MARGIN wallet-derived branch; under MARGIN it takes the OpenPositions branch unconditionally (probe: `quote_currency="ZEUR"` did not filter an XXBT-quoted position). `margin_balance_asset` itself IS read under MARGIN — the client POSTs `TradeBalance` with `asset=ZEUR` (probe-observed body) and uses it solely as the summary display denomination: the TradeBalance metrics dict, the synthetic equity/free-margin balance (`total=e`, `locked=m`, `free=mf` — probe: `1396.79/120.50/1276.29 EUR`), and the single `MarginBalance`. Order submission has no quote-currency dependency (denial guards are TIF/reduce-only/leverage only), and `load_all=True` already loads the XXBT-quoted instruments. One client covers both books.
- **What OpenPositions reports for an XXBT-quoted position: side + base-unit quantity, nothing else** *(confidence: high — real compiled parse path; canned response mirrors Kraken's documented shape)*. Probe: an ETH/XBT sell lot (`vol 4`, `vol_closed 1`) plus a XBT/EUR buy lot → `ETH/BTC.KRAKEN SHORT 3.00000000` and `BTC/EUR.KRAKEN LONG 0.00500000`. `PositionStatusReport` carries no PnL/margin/price fields at all (`avg_px_open=None`), so Kraken's per-position `cost`/`fee`/`margin`/`value`/`net` — denominated in the pair's quote currency, XBT for the /BTC legs — are dropped by the adapter and never reach Nautilus. Instrument normalization measured through the same path: `XETHXXBT` → id `ETH/BTC.KRAKEN` (quote `Currency` code stays `XXBT`), `SOLXBT` → `SOL/BTC.KRAKEN`, `XXBTZEUR` → `BTC/EUR.KRAKEN` (quote `ZEUR`).
- **Kraken's margin engine: one cross-collateralised account-level pool, per the venue's own API shape as held in the repo** *(confidence: medium-high)*. Per-ASSET `collateral_value` haircuts in `/0/public/Assets` (snapshot: ZEUR 1.0, XXBT 0.99, XETH 0.99, SOL 0.925) — collateral is valued at asset level, not per-quote-book; `TradeBalance` returns a single account-wide `e`/`m`/`mf`/`ml` in one display asset ("Kraken reports these values across all collateral" — the adapter's own doc comment); `margin_call 80`/`margin_stop 40` identical on every pair including both /BTC legs (`docs/reference/kraken-snapshot-register.md`). Separate per-pair base-unit exposure caps exist (`long/short_position_limit`: 1000/800 ETH on ETH/BTC, 6900/5100 SOL on SOL/BTC) and the /BTC legs carry the lower 2–5×/2–4× leverage band. **Not establishable offline**: authoritative narrative confirmation from Kraken's margin documentation, and the conversion mechanics/timing by which XBT-book exposure enters the single pool — settled by Kraken's "How margin works" pages (network) or empirically by a tiny-live TradeBalance read with simultaneous EUR- and XBT-quoted positions open.
- **The engine-side work is the actual solve: the EUR assumption lives in the engine's own base-keyed plumbing, not the adapter** *(confidence: high on where; medium on size)*. Concretely: `cli/ohlc/fetch.py::PAIR_KEYS` (symbols are `<BASE>/EUR`); `cli/engine/store.py::PAIR_KEYS` (filters `endswith("/EUR")`, keys by BASE, store path hardcodes `root/<base>/EUR/<interval>.parquet` — base keys collide the moment ETH/EUR and ETH/BTC coexist); `cli/engine/instruments.py::INSTRUMENT_IDS` (`f"{base}/EUR.KRAKEN"`) and `COSTMIN_EUR` (0.45 EUR each — the /BTC legs' costmin is **0.00002 denominated in XBT**, a different unit, the exact trap `feeders.py::load_minimums`' `quote == "EUR"` filter exists to avoid); `cli/engine/cycle.py::_append_orders` (`notional = delta * shadow_nav_eur`, `quantity = |notional| / h4_close` — dimensionally wrong against an XBT-quoted close without an EUR→BTC conversion; order dicts carry `notional_eur`); `cli/engine/venuestate.py` (`VenueState.instruments`/`positions` keyed by base — same collision; `balances` are per-currency and already carry XBT free balance); `tests/test_basket_concordance.py`'s ruled baseline (this topic's mechanical form). `size_order` itself is per-instrument unit-consistent (quote-unit notional vs quote-unit costmin) — the hazard is every consumer reading its notional as EUR. Shape of the solve: a symbol-keyed (base+quote) re-plumb of PAIR_KEYS / store paths / INSTRUMENT_IDS / VenueState, an FX term in sizing, quote-denominated costmin handling, the registry re-ratification through the committed builder, and the concordance-baseline update in the same change — a multi-file iteration on the live trade path (Fable review floor per `spec-plan-locations.md`), not a config edit. Untested and unmeasured here: how Nautilus Portfolio/Cache denominate PnL for an XXBT-quoted fill (today's shadow engine reads neither, so it does not bite until the executor does).
