# Stage 6b sub-phasing — the ratified ladder lands as a §12 amendment

## Context — what

§12's Stage 6b as written (€500–1,000 sleeve, quarter size, weekly tracking error against a 10 bps-of-NAV band) was measured against the real 6a journal on 2026-07-30 and cannot execute: **0 of 801** intended orders clear Kraken's minimums at the tiny-live size as specified. The owner and the session reshaped 6b into a three-rung ladder, and the **shape was ratified 2026-08-02**: rung 1 = the §11 T2 tax probe as the plumbing proof (≥1 margin long + ≥1 margin short across ≥2 rollovers, one closed one settled, ~€10–30); rung 2 = fewer assets at lower budget, time-boxed, operational confidence only — its tracking error is explicitly not the go/no-go number; rung 3 = full weights at €1,000 under the accumulation delta formula ([[T0119]]), gated on a re-derived tracking band ([[T0118]]). This topic is the amendment's carrier: the ladder text, the re-anchored go/no-go band, the §12 edit, and the decision-register entry.

## Why this matters

Without the amendment the ratification exists only in a memo bullet and a conversation — the exact informal drift §12's governance exists to prevent. And the go/no-go gate as pre-registered would measure Kraken's minimums, not our execution: per-asset live-vs-sim mismatch under accumulation is bounded by ~`ordermin` (€30–80 across the active book) while the deployed book at €1,000 is ~€54 — mismatch can exceed deployed exposure, so the written band (≤ €1/week at €1,000) is structurally unmeetable and would force either a false NO-GO or a quiet waiver at the gate.

## Findings so far

- The placeability measurement (2026-07-30, 801 intended orders / 120 cycles / 20 days): €1,000 full weights 4.0 % placeable, €500 full 1.5 %, €1,000 quarter 0.25 %, €500 quarter 0 %; median intended order €0.0116 against 20-day total turnover of ~€275; blocked by `ordermin` (the quantity floor, €3–25/pair in EUR terms — e.g. 20 ADA, 50 DOGE, 3.9 DOT), not `costmin` (€0.45); median miss ~3 orders of magnitude. Scaling: 50 % placeability needs ~€228k, 80 % ~€1.36 M, 95 % ~€9.4 M. The design never produces mostly-placeable orders at any sanctioned size — the ladder is the response, not a workaround.
- Shape ratified 2026-08-02 (owner): parameters wait on two feeder measurements — [[T0117]] (gross decomposition) and [[T0118]] (accumulation replay → the honest band). Both can only move parameters (band width, expected gross, rung-2 scope), not the shape.
- Rung 1 doubles as §11's T2 probe and gives [[T0090]] its first measured fills; [[T0005]]'s T1 check rides alongside; the go/no-go still inherits [[T0064]] and [[T0090]] regardless of staging.
- Caveats on all numbers: journal read from the NAS replica; venue minimums from the snapshot register stamped 2026-07-07 ([[T0113]]); figures describe intent, not fills.

### Both feeder parameters are now measured (2026-08-02, spec `00081`, 136 cycles 2026-07-11 → 2026-08-02)

- **Expected gross for rungs 2–3 = 5.371 % of NAV** (median final gross per cycle; [[T0117]] resolved). The chain that produces it, each ratio a median of per-cycle ratios: A2 sleeve gross **32.224 %** → ÷3 by the **fixed ⅓ combination weights** → **10.741 %** combined → position caps bind on **0 of 136** cycles (no-op) → **×0.5** governor, constant across the window → **5.371 %**. Use the chain, not just the endpoint: three of its four stages are structural constants that would move together if the book's composition or the governor state changed. The naive "15–20 % from a 10–12 % vol target" was never the book's number — a per-sleeve vol target is not a book gross target, and A2 alone runs *above* it.
- **The tracking band, measured as a function of NAV** ([[T0118]] resolved; per cycle, bps of NAV): €500 → median 147.0 / p95 209.2; **€1,000 → 51.2 / 115.7**; €2,500 → 11.6 / 30.9; €5,000 → 6.2 / 14.2; €10,000 → 4.3 / 8.9. Weekly means over the four ISO weeks at €1,000: 94.5 (W28, partial) / 50.3 (W29) / 68.0 (W30) / 32.4 (W31, partial). No weekly p95 exists and none should be quoted — four ISO weeks cannot support a percentile.
- **The pre-registered 10 bps band is unmeetable at rung 3's size by a measured factor**: at €1,000 the venue-imposed floor is **5.1× the band at the median and 11.6× at p95**. It only becomes meetable around **€5,000 (median 6.2) / €10,000 (p95 8.9)** — the far end of §12's ramp, not the tiny-live size the stage specifies. The amendment's re-registered band must be written from these numbers; 10 bps presupposes a book roughly an order of magnitude larger than rung 3's.
- **Re-derivation trigger for the band, carried here because this is where the gate is written**: the floor above is derived from **shadow** targets — what the live book would have traded. If rung 1 or rung 2 execution changes the target series materially — **or the governor lifts off ×0.5, or either dormant sleeve re-arms**, both of which move the targets with no execution change at all — the band wants re-deriving (`zcrypto engine accum-replay` over the then-current journal) **before** rung 3's gate reads it. The regime triggers matter more than the execution one and fail in the dangerous direction: a bigger book shrinks the relative floor, so a band left stale from today's one-sleeve ×0.5 state is too **wide**, and degraded execution would pass a gate it should not. Re-check the minimums stamp in the same pass: the figures rest on `kraken-refdata-20260707T032900Z.json`, `fetched_at 2026-07-07T03:29:00+00:00`, and Kraken moves those without notice ([[T0113]]).
- **[[T0124]] now bears on rung 3's premise, and is an owner decision rather than a parameter.** The same measurement found B and A1 holding **exactly zero in all 136 cycles** — so the ⅓ divisor above is dividing a **one-sleeve** book, and what rung 3 would take live is structurally one sleeve at ⅙ of A2's gross, not the diversified three-sleeve system whose Sharpe/DD/stress figures record 44 registered. Not a defect (both sleeves are long-only and sitting out a downtrend; B non-zero on 51.4 % of the backtest, A1 on 57.0 %), but the ladder's go/no-go should state the divergence knowingly rather than inherit it silently — and a re-arming of either sleeve would move the expected-gross parameter above with nothing announcing it.
