# Phase-6 build sequence and its cross-iteration constraints

## Context — what

The record of Phase 6's build sequence and the todo that is nobody else's: from the kickoff (spec `00039`, iter-079, attended 2026-07-10) through the shadow engine, the VPS deployment, the five-spec 6b sequence (`00088` → `00092`) and master plan §12's three-rung ladder to the go/no-go. The rulings live in `docs/research/14.phase6-decisions.md` under their `[iter-NNN]` tags and are cited here, never restated; each spec holds its own design; the fleet records (`docs/reference/deploy-log.jsonl`, `fleet-pins.md`, `drill-log.md`) hold what was converged and drilled. This file holds the sequence's state in one table, the constraints that bind across iterations, what landed and when, and the still-open steps with their owner, date, procedure and precondition.

## Why this matters

The sequence spans more than twenty iterations and three months, and the step that binds the next one is invisible at pick time unless written where the next session reads: a spec started before its predecessor is converged, a converge on the primary inside rung 2's box, or a rung entered without its precondition each costs real money or unbackfillable state. A committed spec is not a durable home for deferred actions, so they are owed here until a topic, a plan task or a procedure owns them.
