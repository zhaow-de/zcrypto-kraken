# Stage 6b sub-phasing — the ratified ladder lands as a §12 amendment

## Context — what

§12's Stage 6b as written (€500–1,000 sleeve, quarter size, weekly tracking error against a 10 bps-of-NAV band) was measured against the real 6a journal on 2026-07-30 and cannot execute: **0 of 801** intended orders clear Kraken's minimums at the tiny-live size as specified. The owner and the session reshaped 6b into a three-rung ladder, and the **shape was ratified 2026-08-02**: rung 1 = the §11 T2 tax probe as the plumbing proof (≥1 margin long + ≥1 margin short across ≥2 rollovers, one closed one settled, ~€10–30); rung 2 = fewer assets at lower budget, time-boxed, operational confidence only — its tracking error is explicitly not the go/no-go number; rung 3 = full weights at €1,000 under the accumulation delta formula ([[T0119]]), gated on a re-derived tracking band ([[T0118]]). This topic is the amendment's carrier: the ladder text, the re-anchored go/no-go band, the §12 edit, and the decision-register entry.

## Why this matters

Without the amendment the ratification exists only in a memo bullet and a conversation — the exact informal drift §12's governance exists to prevent. And the go/no-go gate as pre-registered would measure Kraken's minimums, not our execution: per-asset live-vs-sim mismatch under accumulation is bounded by ~`ordermin` (€30–80 across the active book) while the deployed book at €1,000 is ~€54 — mismatch can exceed deployed exposure, so the written band (≤ €1/week at €1,000) is structurally unmeetable and would force either a false NO-GO or a quiet waiver at the gate.

- **The tracking band, measured as a function of NAV** ([[T0118]] resolved; per cycle, bps of NAV): €500 → median 147.0 / p95 209.2; **€1,000 → 51.2 / 115.7**; €2,500 → 11.6 / 30.9; €5,000 → 6.2 / 14.2; €10,000 → 4.3 / 8.9. - **The pre-registered 10 bps band is unmeetable at rung 3's size by a measured factor**: at €1,000 the venue-imposed floor is **5.1× the band at the median and 11.6× at p95**. It only becomes meetable around **€5,000 (median 6.2) / €10,000 (p95 8.9)** — the far end of §12's ramp, not the tiny-live size the stage specifies. The amendment's re-registered band must be written from these numbers; 10 bps presupposes a book roughly an order of magnitude larger than rung 3's.
- **Re-derivation trigger for the band, carried here because this is where the gate is written**: the floor above is derived from **shadow** targets — what the live book would have traded. If rung 1 or rung 2 execution changes the target series materially — **or the governor lifts off ×0.5, or either dormant sleeve re-arms**, both of which move the targets with no execution change at all — the band wants re-deriving (`zcrypto engine accum-replay` over the then-current journal) **before** rung 3's gate reads it. The regime triggers matter more than the execution one and fail in the dangerous direction: a bigger book shrinks the relative floor, so a band left stale from today's one-sleeve ×0.5 state is too **wide**, and degraded execution would pass a gate it should not. Re-check the minimums stamp in the same pass: the figures rest on `kraken-refdata-20260707T032900Z.json`, `fetched_at 2026-07-07T03:29:00+00:00`, and Kraken moves those without notice ([[T0113]]).

## Resolution

**Ratified and landed 2026-08-02.** The amendment is in `docs/research/00.master-plan.md` §12 — Stage 6b's and the go/no-go's lines **rewritten in place** (822 → 3,245 chars), not corrected by an appended note: a master plan carrying the original text with a retraction below it reads wrong cold, because the uncorrected version reads first and more confidently. The superseded single-step form is stated as measured-unexecutable rather than deleted silently, so a reader learns why it changed.

What §12 now says: the **three rungs** with their entry/exit criteria (rung 1 = the §11 T2 probe as the plumbing proof; rung 2 = time-boxed operational confidence with a skip-or-carry policy fixed at entry and **its tracking error explicitly excluded from the gate**; rung 3 = full weights at €1,000 under `delta = target − actually held`, with its four preconditions named); the go/no-go reading **the measured band at the funded NAV** instead of the unmeetable 10 bps — with the **edge pinned as the p95 of the per-cycle drift floor against that week's mean drift, over ≥3 COMPLETE ISO weeks** (review caught the first draft naming no edge at all: on the very data the band was derived from, a median edge fails two of four weeks and a p95 edge passes all four, so the unstated choice flipped the gate outright) — with the re-derivation trigger that fires on a governor lift, a sleeve re-arming, or **any ramp step** (the floor is a function of funded NAV) — not only on execution changes — because a stale band is too *wide* and would pass execution it should fail; performance judged against the **conditional** expectation as a band with its CI, never a point and never the headline record figure; and [[T0124]]'s composition divergence **stated knowingly**, with sleeve occupancy a rung-3 precondition.

The concrete figures are deliberately **not** in §12 — 5.371 % expected gross, 51.2/115.7 bps at €1,000 with its full NAV curve, the ≈0.5–0.75 conditional band — because §12 is the north-star narrative and a number quoted there is quoted for years, while each of these moves the moment the band is re-derived. They live in the Findings above and in the resolved [[T0117]] / [[T0118]]. The decision-register entry (`14.phase6-decisions.md`, `[iter-118]`) carries the reasoning and the rejected framings; `14.phase6b-session-brief.md`'s decision queue now says plainly which of its items 1–6 the amendment overtook and which stand.

**What is NOT resolved by this and must not be read as settled**: the rungs are ratified, not executed — [[T0124]]'s sleeve-occupancy gauge is built but dark until an attended converge ships it, so rung 3's precondition is undischarged; [[T0121]]'s §10 whole-book limits are still unwired; [[T0119]]'s accumulation formula is still unimplemented in the executor; and the go/no-go continues to inherit [[T0090]] and [[T0064]] exactly as before.
