## 2026-07-08 — iter-024: explicit-cost backtester engine (Phase 3)

- **Opened `cli/backtest/`** (Phase 3 kickoff) — `run_backtest(asset_returns, positions, *, fee_rate, periods_per_year)`: turns a strategy's target positions into a **net-return series** after explicit per-turnover fees, then scores it. Reuses `cli.validation.{sharpe, max_drawdown, annualized_return}` (not reimplemented) → `{net_returns, total_return, sharpe, max_drawdown, annualized_return, n_periods}`. **15 tests** (full suite 425). Stdlib-only; the engine the benchmark family B0–B4 is built on.
- **Timing convention (fixed, look-ahead-critical):** `positions[t]` is held during period `t` and earns `asset_returns[t]` (no index shift); the caller must set `positions[t]` from information ≤ `t−1` — documented as the strategy's obligation (the engine can't enforce it; the CPCV/validation harness is what catches a leaky strategy). `turnover[t] = |positions[t] − positions[t−1]|` with `positions[−1]=0`, so the initial entry from flat is charged once; a full sign flip costs turnover 2 (trade through flat).
- **Never NaN.** A **flat** strategy (zero-variance net → Sharpe undefined) and a **blow-up** (`1 + net[t] ≤ 0`) both surface as `BacktestError` (wrapping the metrics' `ValidationError`), and `total_return` is computed only after the metrics validate — no NaN, no leaked `ValidationError`.
- **Distrust-the-instrument.** The whole-branch review hand-worked **and** live-ran the buy-and-hold (bit-for-bit `net=[0.09,−0.05,0.20]`, entry fee once) + sign-flip (turnover 2) cases, confirmed the metrics are genuinely reused (compared to the harness directly), and that the timing test genuinely pins index alignment (distinct returns ⇒ an off-by-one shift would fail). No look-ahead, no cost double-count.
- **Deferred (v1 scope):** margin carry (`cli.costs.margin_carry`) + spread (T0003-gated) + borrow as parameters; multi-asset/portfolio (the basket benchmark); cost-stress multipliers (§9.6, applied at eval time); the benchmark strategies B0–B4 (next iterations).
- **Design/plan:** `docs/specs/00016-backtester-engine-design.md`, `docs/plans/00016-backtester-engine.md`. First Phase-3 component.
- Independent whole-branch review (Sonnet): APPROVED, no Critical/Important findings — timing convention verified free of look-ahead, cost accounting correct, metrics reused not reimplemented, never NaN.
