**Goal:** A ruled research subject `p1-reprice` that rebuilds record 47's book over `ohlc-full` at the `fee_per_side` and `spread_per_side` the owner picks, re-computes its registered figures there against the benchmark charged the same, reads them against the benchmark-relative bar record 47 passed, and lands its run as a P1 trial (family count 7) on `docs/reference/trial-registry.jsonl`, its rule fixed by spec 00154's sha256 — the pick written into the spec before the hash.

  - `class RepricingError(Exception)`.
  - `refuse_past_in_sample(daily_ts: list[datetime], h4_ts: list[datetime]) -> None` — `RepricingError(f"its last 4h bar, {h4_ts[-1]}, lies past {REFERENCE_LAST_BAR}, the in-sample end")` when `h4_ts[-1] > REFERENCE_LAST_BAR`, else the same form for the daily grid when `daily_ts[-1] > LAST_DAILY_BAR`.

  - `live_state_returns(result: CrossfreqSystemResult) -> list[int]` — the `k` in `range(result.n_periods)` at which `all(p[a][k] == 0.0 for a in p)` for `p` each of `result.sleeve_positions["B"]` and `["A1"]`, and `any(result.sleeve_positions["A2"][a][k] != 0.0 for a in …)`.
  - `conditional_live(governed: list[float], live: list[int]) -> float` — `0.0` when `len(live) < 2` or the `governed[k]` over `live` are all equal, else `float(sharpe([governed[k] for k in live], periods_per_year=PPY_4H))`.
  - `multipliers_moved(registered: CrossfreqSystemResult, basis: CrossfreqSystemResult) -> int` — `sum(1 for k in range(basis.n_periods) if basis.multipliers[k] != registered.multipliers[k])`.
  - `spa_cells(book: list[float], bench: list[float]) -> dict[str, float]` — over `outperformance = [[b - m] for b, m in zip(book, bench, strict=True)]`: `spa_p_full` at `(SPA_HEADLINE_BLOCK, SPA_HEADLINE_SEED)` over every row, `spa_p_decisive` at the same cell over `outperformance[DECISIVE_START:]`, and `spa_grid_b{block}_s{seed}` for each `(block, seed)` of `SPA_GRID` over the decisive rows; each `float(reality_check_pvalue(matrix, mean_block=block, n_resamples=N_RESAMPLES, seed=seed)["p_value"])` — seven keys.
  - `bar_flags(*, dsr: float, spa: dict[str, float], stress_15: float, beats_worst: bool) -> dict[str, int]` — `dsr_pass = int(dsr > DSR_PASS_THRESHOLD)`; `spa_pass = int(max(v for k, v in spa.items() if k != "spa_p_full") < SPA_PASS_BELOW)`; `cost_stress_pass = int(stress_15 > 0)`; `worst_slice_relative_pass = int(beats_worst)`; `passes = int(all four)`.
  - `METRIC_KEYS: frozenset[str]` = `{"fee_per_side", "spread_per_side", "cost_per_side", "registered_basis_returns", "registered_basis_sharpe", "ann_sharpe_noc", "ann_sharpe_noc_decisive", "per_period_sharpe_4h", "bench4h_sharpe_full", "bench4h_sharpe_decisive", "cap_breach_bars", "governor_engaged_bars", "maxdd", "maxdd_pre_governor", "cost_stress_1_5x_sharpe_ann", "cost_stress_2x_sharpe_ann", "var_trials_4h", "dsr", "spa_p_full", "spa_p_decisive", "spa_grid_b30_s7", "spa_grid_b30_s1234", "spa_grid_b102_s42", "spa_grid_b102_s7", "spa_grid_b102_s1234", "dsr_pass", "spa_pass", "cost_stress_pass", "worst_slice_relative_pass", "passes", "worst_book_sharpe", "worst_benchmark_sharpe", "conditional_live_sharpe", "conditional_live_bars", "multipliers_moved"}`.
  - `repricing_metrics(daily_ts, daily_prices, h4_ts, h4_prices, *, registered: CrossfreqSystemConfig, basis: CrossfreqSystemConfig, n_trials: int) -> dict` — in this order: `refuse_past_in_sample(daily_ts, h4_ts)`, before any build; `reg` and `bas`, `build_crossfreq_system_fast(daily_prices, daily_ts, h4_prices, h4_ts, config=…)` at `registered` and at `basis`; the two stress builds at `stressed_config(basis, m)` for `m` in `STRESS_MULTIPLIERS`; `bench = benchmark_4h_net_of_cost(h4_prices, cost_per_side=basis.cost_per_side)`, called once; `g = bas.governed_net`. Then exactly `METRIC_KEYS`: the basis's three terms (`cost_per_side` the property); `registered_basis_returns = reg.n_periods`, `registered_basis_sharpe = sharpe(reg.governed_net, periods_per_year=PPY_4H)`; `ann_sharpe_noc`, `ann_sharpe_noc_decisive` (`g[DECISIVE_START:]`), `per_period_sharpe_4h = sharpe(g)`; `bench4h_sharpe_full`, `bench4h_sharpe_decisive` likewise over `bench`; `cap_breach_bars`, `governor_engaged_bars` of `bas`; `maxdd = max_drawdown(g)`, `maxdd_pre_governor = max_drawdown(bas.ungoverned_net)`; each stress figure the annualized Sharpe of its build's `governed_net`; `var_trials_4h = P1_VAR_TRIALS`; `dsr = deflated_sharpe_ratio(per_period_sharpe_4h, len(g), n_trials=n_trials, var_trials=P1_VAR_TRIALS)`; `spa_cells(g, bench)`; `relative = benchmark_relative_worst_slice(calendar_year_slices(g, h4_ts), calendar_year_slices(bench, h4_ts))`, `worst_book_sharpe` and `worst_benchmark_sharpe` its two keys of those names; `bar_flags(dsr=…, spa=…, stress_15=cost_stress_1_5x_sharpe_ann, beats_worst=relative["beats_benchmark_worst"])`; `conditional_live_sharpe = conditional_live(g, live)`, `conditional_live_bars = len(live)` for `live = live_state_returns(bas)`; `multipliers_moved(reg, bas)`.
