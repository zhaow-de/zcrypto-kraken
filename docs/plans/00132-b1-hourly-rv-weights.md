### Task 1 — the hourly realized-variance inverse weights

**Files:** `cli/alpha/b1_rv.py` (new), `tests/test_alpha_b1_rv.py` (new).

- [ ] **Step 1 — the failing tests**, synthetic series, built by a test-side helper `_union_and_hourly(paths, start, ...)` that takes each asset's hourly close path (a list over consecutive hours, `None` for a missing hour), writes the hourly series of the present hours, and derives each 4h close as the close of the last present hourly bar in `[T, T + 4h)` — the measured seam (spec, the measured basis) — returning `union_ts` (bar-open stamps, tz-aware UTC), `prices_by_asset` on the union (`None` where an asset has no 4h bar) and `hourly_by_asset` (`{asset: (stamps, closes)}`); the fixtures use `lookback=4` so the windows are short enough to plant in, except where named:
  - **planted realized variance**: BTC and two other assets, every hourly log return of each asset of that asset's own magnitude `m` (closes chained as `c[i] = c[i − 1] · exp(±m)`, the signs varied by bucket so no 4h window is flat and arm A weights all three, the three magnitudes distinct); at a stamp `t` the weights equal `{asset: (1 / s_asset) / Σ (1 / s)}` with `s_asset = sqrt(n_t · m_asset² / 4)`, `n_t` the test's own count of hourly bars in the window and `m` the asset's magnitude, compared with `math.isclose(rel_tol=1e-12)` asset by asset, and the stamp's weights sum to 1 within 1e-15;
  - **the window's bounds**: with a step `δ` planted by multiplying an asset's closes by `exp(δ)` from one bar on — which changes that bar's log return alone — at the bar closing exactly at `union_ts[t − 4] + 4h` the weights at `t` equal the unplanted run's; at the bar one hour later, and at the bar closing at `union_ts[t] + 4h`, they differ; at the bar one hour after that, the weights at `t` equal the unplanted run's and those at `t + 1` differ;
  - **no look-ahead, the truncating prefix**: for every `n` from `lookback + 1` to the last return index, the weights over `union_ts[: n + 1]`, the prices' first `n + 1` entries and only the hourly bars whose close stamp `h + timedelta(hours=1)` is at or before `union_ts[n − 1] + timedelta(hours=4)` — the cutoff computed by the test from its own stamps, never from the module — equal the full run's first `n`; the fixture holds hourly bars after every cutoff, so reading one hour past the decision boundary changes a value;
  - **gap returns**: an asset missing two consecutive hours inside the window, the bar after the gap carrying a planted `δ` relative to the bar before it, and missing the hour before the window's lower edge, so its first windowed bar's predecessor closes before `union_ts[t − 4] + 4h`: the weights at `t` equal those the test computes from `math.fsum` of its own list of the window's squared log returns, the gap's return `ln(c_after / c_before)` counted once and the edge bar's return reaching back to its predecessor;
  - **eligibility is arm A's**: on a fixture with a late-listed asset, an asset missing one 4h bar mid-history (its hourly bars for that bucket absent too), an asset flat for a whole window, and BTC absent at one stamp, forward-filled by `cli.portfolio.crossfreq_system._ffill_btc` before both calls (its hourly bars for that bucket absent), `[set(w) for w in hourly_rv_inverse_vol_weights(fed, union_ts, hourly, lookback=4)] == [set(w) for w in _inverse_vol_weights(fed, lookback=4)]` at every return index, and some index weights fewer assets than it has present;
  - **the refusals**, each its own case, each `AlphaError` naming the asset and the stamp, the seam broken by hand for these three: an asset arm A weights at `t` whose window holds no hourly bar; one whose first windowed bar has no predecessor row (its hourly series starting inside the window); one whose hourly closes are flat over the window while its 4h closes move (arm A's stdev above 0, `rv` 0). Input refusals, parametrised, each `AlphaError`: hourly stamps not strictly increasing; a tz-naive hourly stamp; a close of 0, of `nan`, of `None`; an hourly asset set other than the price assets; `union_ts` not strictly increasing; a tz-naive `union_ts` stamp; `union_ts` of a length other than the price lists'.
